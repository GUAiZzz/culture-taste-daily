Reformation 把店里的地毯带回家

中文

Reformation 门店里的地毯，过去是品牌空间的一部分，却不是顾客可以买回家的商品。11 月 9 日，品牌与法国地毯工作室 Les Éditions de Tapis 发布九款限量、按订单制作的地毯，把这段门店合作延伸到家居。官方商品页将它们放进同一个系列；Vogue 与 Architectural Digest 的采访则把合作追溯到双方为门店共同设计地毯的五年。 系列页面 · Vogue

据 Cohan 在 Architectural Digest 的采访中所说，顾客在店里看到地毯后，曾询问能否购买。Reformation 创意总监 Lauren Caris Cohan 与工作室创办人 Thomas Coccimiglio 因此把原本服务于零售空间的设计，重新组织成家用系列。棕榈、天鹅、鸟与猫等手绘形象搭配边饰和留白，让地毯既是房间里的实用物件，也成为一幅铺在地上的图。 Architectural Digest

设计所说的巴黎与洛杉矶，也不是一项抽象的城市标签。Coccimiglio 在 Vogue 采访中把它解释为两种彼此想象的文化：法国人想象洛杉矶的电影、摇滚与魅力，美国人则想象法国历史与宫廷。Architectural Digest 记录了其中的具体图案选择——交错的棕榈、天鹅和粉蓝色——以及创作者如何回看多年门店设计。系列由此把品牌熟悉的店内视觉，转成可辨认但不完全重复的家居语言。 Vogue · Architectural Digest

每款从手绘稿开始，再由印度和尼泊尔工匠手工打结；材料包括羊毛、亚麻与丝。品牌的 Swans Fans 5 × 7 英尺款标价 1,200 美元，商品页列出的预估交付周期为购买后约四至六周。它们是高价、限量、按单制作的物件，现有材料不足以说明这种做法的环境影响，也不能据此推断 Reformation 将持续扩展家居业务。Forbes Vetted 转述品牌称目前没有进一步进入家居品类的确定计划。 系列页面 · Swans Fans 商品页 · Forbes Vetted

地毯原先出现的空间，也能说明这段合作的来处。2023 年纽约 SoHo 门店在一栋 1881 年建筑里混合新旧元素，试衣间配备触屏；2026 年巴黎门店则把手结地毯放在陶土砖、木饰面与 Murano 玻璃之间。两家店的设计不同，却都把衣服放进一套完整的室内场景。Retail Dive 的 SoHo 门店报道 · PalaceScope 的巴黎门店介绍

Coccimiglio 在一段较早的设计师访谈中说，他会从想象中的地点或故事开始画草图，也会考虑一件作品如何进入现有室内。这是他对一般工作方法的描述，并非这次系列每款图案的制作记录。设计师访谈

所以，这次发布更适合被看作一次空间身份的商品化：顾客把门店记忆带回家，品牌则把服装之外的设计语言变成可购买的对象。值得留意的是，一件店内物经由顾客的询问，获得了新的使用场景；它能否成为长期产品线，仍需后续事实回答。

The rugs inside Reformation stores have been part of the brand’s spaces, but were not previously products customers could take home. On November 9, the brand and French rug studio Les Éditions de Tapis released nine limited, made-to-order designs, extending their retail collaboration into the home. The official collection page presents them as one range; interviews in Vogue and Architectural Digest trace the partnership to five years of designing rugs for Reformation stores. 系列页面 · Vogue

According to Cohan’s interview with Architectural Digest, shoppers saw the rugs in stores and asked whether they could buy them. Reformation chief creative officer Lauren Caris Cohan and studio founder Thomas Coccimiglio then reshaped work originally made for retail spaces into a collection for homes. Hand-drawn palms, swans, birds, and a cat sit alongside borders and open areas, making each rug both a useful object and an image laid across the room. Architectural Digest

The Paris and Los Angeles references are more than abstract city labels. In Vogue, Coccimiglio describes two cultures imagining one another: French ideas of Los Angeles through cinema, rock ’n’ roll, and glamour, and American ideas of French history and court life. Architectural Digest records specific choices—crossed palms, swans, and pink and blue—and how the designers revisited years of store designs. The collection turns a familiar in-store visual language into something recognizable without simply repeating it. Vogue · Architectural Digest

Each design begins as a drawing and is hand-knotted by artisans in India and Nepal, using materials that include wool, linen, and silk. The brand lists the 5 × 7-foot Swans Fans rug at $1,200 and estimates a lead time of four to six weeks from purchase. These are expensive, limited, made-to-order objects. The available evidence does not establish their environmental impact, nor does it show that Reformation will keep expanding into home goods. Forbes Vetted reports that the brand has no firm plans for further home-category expansion. 系列页面 · Swans Fans 商品页 · Forbes Vetted

The spaces where the rugs first appeared help explain the collaboration. The 2023 SoHo flagship mixed contemporary and vintage elements inside an 1881 building, with touchscreens in its fitting rooms. A 2026 account of the Paris store describes hand-knotted rugs alongside terracotta tiles, woodwork and Murano glass. The two stores differ, but both put clothing inside a complete interior scene. Retail Dive on SoHo · PalaceScope on Paris

In an earlier interview, Coccimiglio described starting with an imagined place or story, sketching from it, and considering how an object might sit within an existing interior. It describes his general process, not the making of every design in this release. Designer interview

The launch is best read as the commercialization of a spatial identity: customers can take a memory of the stores home, while the brand turns a design language beyond clothing into an object for sale. A store object gained a new setting through customers’ questions. Whether it becomes a lasting product line remains an open question.

Stella McCartney 与奈良美智的第三次合作

中文

Stella McCartney 与奈良美智的第三次合作，品牌英文页面预告 11 月 11 日在官网及部分精品店发售，具体地区与渠道仍需核对。品牌把它命名为「Rock’n’Roll Forest」，并称这是把奈良的角色带入时装语境、连接艺术、时尚与音乐的一章。眼下可确认的内容，是成衣、手袋、鞋与配饰组成的胶囊系列；品牌语言所说的“可穿戴艺术”，最终要落到这些具体物件上看。 Stella McCartney

这次联名跨越了印花、手工针织与鞋履。ELLE 日本报道提到四件在品牌工坊制作的手工作品，其中一条手织围巾约需 40 小时；另有采用刺绣图案的针织衫。鞋款则加入 adidas，基于 Tokyo 64 田径鞋重新设计。此处需要保留品类差异：一条围巾的制作时间不能代表整组系列，鞋款也属于三方合作，而非仅由 Stella 与奈良共同完成。 ELLE Japan · Artsy

品牌用「Rock’n’Roll Forest」将艺术家的角色、服装与音乐放在同一叙事里。比起把这套说法直接等同于艺术家的全部创作，值得关注的是图像怎样进入不同物件：从印花到刺绣、针织和鞋履，制作方式改变了它被穿着和观看的方式。这个判断来自系列呈现，而不是对艺术家意图的推测。

音乐也有一段更长的创作背景。洛杉矶郡艺术博物馆在回顾 1984 至 2020 年作品的展览指南中，以音乐作为理解奈良绘画和雕塑的一条线索；他为《My Drawing Room》选过一组歌曲。这解释了为什么音乐适合成为观看其作品的入口，不能替代艺术家对本次胶囊系列的具体说明。LACMA 展览指南

伊势丹新宿列出的活动时间为 11 月 11 日至 12 月 8 日，首日上午抽签决定入场资格，下午抽签决定入场顺序。具体单品与入场规则应以店方公告为准，也不能把新宿的时间自动套到其他地区。伊势丹新宿公告

在日本，ELLE 还预告了伊势丹新宿和阪急梅田的限时空间，报道说会使用再利用展具、纸和纸板装置，并播放双方策划的歌单。这些活动呈现的是此次联名的线下体验，不足以证明全球各地会采用相同设置。品牌把衣服、手作、音乐和空间放进同一传播场景，强化了联名的可体验性；它是否让奈良的图像获得新的观看方式，还要等作品与实际陈列出现后再判断。 ELLE Japan · 伊势丹新宿

西岸艺术与设计博览会的滨江现场

中文

西岸艺博会开幕：一座城市如何把艺术放进不同尺度的空间

2026 年西岸艺术与设计博览会于 11 月 10 日在上海启动，活动延续至 15 日；公众开放时间为 12 日至 15 日。四个场地、150 多家参展机构构成这一届的基本规模，但更值得看的不是数字本身，而是作品如何在美术馆、画廊展位和城市文化空间之间移动。上海市政府介绍；官方活动日程。

在画廊的安排中，这种差异已经显现。Hauser & Wirth 的展位把 Louise Bourgeois、张恩利等艺术家的作品与出版物放在同一场景；亚洲艺术中心则带来十位艺术家的作品。这些安排让人看到，画廊如何在有限空间中组织不同代际和媒介。Hauser & Wirth 展位介绍。

材料也改变了观看的速度。Lighthouse Kanata 展出冨樫葉子的玻璃作品；画廊的 《Bloom Softly 2-26》 作品页记录了吹制、切割玻璃的材料和工艺。Qualia Gallery 则将 Ru Xiaofan 和 Yulia Pinkusevich 的作品放入自己的展示脉络。走过展位时，可以把它们当作不同观看方式的提案，而不只是待辨认的名字。 冨樫在 Homo Faber 的访谈中说，她的工作结合玻璃受热流动的瞬间与冷却后雕琢、研磨的时间；这是她对自身工作方法的解释。

本届展位也出现了一次变动。米兰 Wizard Gallery 在 11 月 7 日声明中称，参展计划里的七件 Diango Hernández 作品无法展出，画廊因此决定退出。Artribune 的采访记录了画廊主对撤展和成本的说明。画廊提出的原因尚未获得主管部门或博览会的独立回应，不能据此断定完整经过。

回看往届，博览会里的媒介和商业环境也在变化。2022 年，《解放日报》的报道记录西岸“视角”单元的一场数字艺术展，涉及 15 位中国艺术家的 19 件作品。2024 年，法国《世界报》采访当时同时参加西岸的画廊主 Magda Danysz，记录了她对当时买家信心的担忧。这是一位画廊主对当时市场的观察。它们提醒我们，艺术周既是文化现场，也在持续面对媒介和市场的变化。

西岸的意义也不能只由现场成交衡量。2019 年关于艺博会的研究曾讨论教育和公共参与在博览会中的位置。对于今年，更具体的问题是：博览会结束之后，这些短暂相遇能否继续变成看展、学习和与城市发生关系的习惯。

West Bund opens: art across the scales of a city

West Bund Art & Design began in Shanghai on November 10 and runs through November 15, with public admission from November 12–15. Across four venues and more than 150 participating organisations, the fair offers a chance to see how art moves between gallery booths, cultural institutions and the wider city. Shanghai city overview; official programme.

The booths propose different ways of assembling a scene. Hauser & Wirth brings together works by artists including Louise Bourgeois and Zhang Enli alongside publications; Asia Art Center presents ten artists. Neither is a complete picture of the field, but each shows how a gallery makes connections across generations and media within a limited space.

Material changes the pace of looking, too. Lighthouse Kanata presents glass by Yoko Togashi; its page for Bloom Softly 2-26 identifies the medium as blown and cut glass. Qualia Gallery places works by Ru Xiaofan and Yulia Pinkusevich in another context. In an interview with Homo Faber, Togashi describes her practice as bringing together the moment glass moves under heat and the slower work of carving and polishing it after cooling. This is her account of her wider practice.

One planned booth has also changed. In a November 7 statement, Milan’s Wizard Gallery said seven works by Diango Hernández could not be shown and that it had consequently withdrawn. Artribune interviewed the gallery owner about the withdrawal and its costs. The gallery’s explanation has not been independently confirmed by the fair or the relevant authority, so the full circumstances remain unresolved.

Earlier editions show how both media and commercial conditions change. In 2022, Jiefang Daily reported on a digital-art exhibition in West Bund’s Perspective section, involving 19 works by 15 Chinese artists. That was a specific past exhibition, not evidence that the same programme is running this year. In 2024, Le Monde interviewed gallerist Magda Danysz, then participating in West Bund, about her concerns over buyer confidence. Her individual observation cannot establish this year’s sales. Together, the historical accounts suggest that art week is a cultural meeting point shaped by changing media and market conditions.

A fair's value cannot be reduced to sales. A 2019 study of art-fair innovation discussed education and public participation, though it says nothing about this year's results. The question for West Bund is what remains after the fair: whether a brief encounter becomes a habit of looking, learning and engaging with the city.

Leon Xu：在城市日常与记忆之间作画

中文
