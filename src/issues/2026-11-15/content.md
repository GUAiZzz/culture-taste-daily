# 材料怎样成为作品

HOW MATERIAL BECOMES A WORK

一件熟悉的家具换了材料，一张照片换了载体，一首新歌让演奏与文字重新面对彼此。今天沿着服装、唱片、展位与房间，去看作品具体怎样被做成，又怎样进入观看与使用。

本期采用实际研究与锁定时间。已经发行、正在展出、计划收官与尚待发生的安排，在各篇中分别记录。

## 家具回到制作现场，Molteni让熟悉的形式重新长出问题

### 中文

本周在 Giussano 新开的《Materialized: Ideas in Matter》，把 Molteni&C、UniFor 和 Citterio 已有的家具交回设计师手里。Ron Gilad 策划的题目很具体：保留一个熟悉的物件，再改变它的材料、比例或用途。官方展览页将展期起点列为11月12日，首页另写13日开幕；本期按本周新展开幕来阅读，不把两个时间写成同一种公众入场安排。[展览说明](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/exhibition)

Herzog & de Meuron 给 Porta Volta 换上透明的充气坐垫与靠背。木结构仍然在，原本遮住一部分结构的柔软部件变得可以看穿。深泽直人的 Cinnamon 则往另一个方向走：藤编要把平面的编织变成立体、能包住身体的曲面。官方作品说明特别强调熟练工匠的编织知识。两张椅子一起看，材料改变了结构如何被看见，也改变了身体会怎样想象坐下。[Porta Volta](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/porta-volta-special-edition-2026) · [Cinnamon](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/cinnamon-special-edition-2026)

Jean Nouvel 的两件作品通过反射发生联系。Less 的薄桌面成为收进建筑、光线与人影的镜面；Elémentaire 用镜面皮革重新解释扶手椅。这里的“几乎没有”是设计师的命题，物件仍然要被制作、支撑和使用，不能把视觉上的轻误读为物理上没有重量。[Less](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/less-special-edition-2026) · [Elémentaire](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/elementaire-special-edition-2026)

Banco 把厨房台面带离墙边。四只水槽和四个水龙头组成循环，像城市广场中的喷泉。它延伸了 Luca Meda 原设计的独立性，也让日常工作的台面进入雕塑的观看方式。官方说明能支持这件作品的布置和比喻，不能证明它适合家中的日常厨房。[Banco](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/banco-special-edition-2026)

原设计也值得回头看。Patricia Urquiola 的工作室记录，2004年的 Diamond 用折叠的铝和内部构件形成桌腿；这提供了一个材料与形态共同工作的旧起点。2018年，Gilad 向 Indesign 解释 Teorema 如何通过开槽、弯折和连续木皮制造看似简单的盒体。读这些旧资料，是为了把新变化放回制作问题里，不能把旧访谈写成他对本次展览的发言。[Diamond 原设计](https://patriciaurquiola.com/product/diamond) · [Gilad，2018年访谈](https://www.indesignlive.com/articles/5-minutes-ron-gilad)

Wallpaper* 此次采访 Gilad 时，他谈到展览给了自己暂时离开商业可行性约束的空间。Elle Decor 的当期报道则把注意力放在 AMDL CIRCLE 的 MDL System 上：重复的桌腿让静止物件带出行走感。一个说的是生产条件，另一个说的是观看结果，二者并读才让“实验”变得具体。[Wallpaper*，11月13日](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/molteni-museum-materialized-ideas-in-matter-ron-gilad) · [Elle Decor，11月13日](https://www.elledecor.com/it/design/a74419502/mostra-molteni-museum-a-giussano/)

这些材料主要来自品牌博物馆、设计师说明及设计媒体。它们足以展示物件如何被重新制造、解释，尚不能说明观众如何使用、市场是否接受，或行业正在整体转向。展览的可看之处，就落在这个还没有被销售结果填满的阶段。

### English

Materialized: Ideas in Matter opened this week in Giussano, returning familiar furniture from Molteni&C, UniFor and Citterio to designers for another round of interpretation. Curated by Ron Gilad, the exhibition asks what can change when an existing object is given another material, proportion or use. The exhibition page begins its dates on November 12, while the museum homepage names November 13 as the opening. Those entries should not be collapsed into a single claim about public admission. [Exhibition](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/exhibition)

Herzog & de Meuron pair Porta Volta’s wooden elements with transparent inflatable cushions. More of the chair’s construction becomes visible through the soft components. Naoto Fukasawa’s Cinnamon takes a different route: rattan weaving turns a flat technique into a three-dimensional surface that receives the body. Its official account stresses the knowledge of skilled artisans. Together, the chairs suggest how material changes both the visibility of construction and the way a person imagines sitting. [Porta Volta](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/porta-volta-special-edition-2026) · [Cinnamon](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/cinnamon-special-edition-2026)

Reflection connects Jean Nouvel’s two works. Less becomes a thin mirrored surface that gathers architecture, passing silhouettes and light. Elémentaire uses mirrored leather to reinterpret an armchair. “Almost nothing” is the designer’s proposition; the objects still require material, support and manufacture. Apparent lightness should not be confused with a physical absence of weight. [Less](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/less-special-edition-2026) · [Elémentaire](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/elementaire-special-edition-2026)

Banco moves the kitchen counter away from surrounding walls. Four sinks and four faucets form a circular arrangement reminiscent of a fountain in a city square. The work extends the independence of Luca Meda’s original design and invites a sculptural reading of a working surface. The official description establishes this arrangement and analogy, rather than proving its suitability for an ordinary domestic kitchen. [Banco](https://www.moltenigroup.com/museum/en/product/banco-special-edition-2026)

Earlier work gives the changes a useful material history. Patricia Urquiola’s studio describes Diamond, designed in 2004, through its folded aluminium legs and internal components. In a 2018 interview with Indesign, Gilad explained the grooves, folds and continuous veneer behind Teorema’s apparently simple boxes. These records locate experimentation in manufacturing; the older interview is not a statement about the present show. [Diamond](https://patriciaurquiola.com/product/diamond) · [2018 interview](https://www.indesignlive.com/articles/5-minutes-ron-gilad)

In Wallpaper’s new interview, Gilad discusses the freedom to work without immediately satisfying commercial viability. Elle Decor’s current account examines how the repeated legs of AMDL CIRCLE’s MDL System suggest movement in a stationary object. One describes conditions of production; the other considers an effect of looking. [Wallpaper](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/molteni-museum-materialized-ideas-in-matter-ron-gilad) · [Elle Decor](https://www.elledecor.com/it/design/a74419502/mostra-molteni-museum-a-giussano/)

The evidence comes mainly from the company museum, designers and design publications. It can establish how these objects were remade and framed. It cannot establish audience use, market acceptance or a shift across the whole industry. The exhibition remains interesting precisely at this stage, before a sales result settles the story.

## Nike 与 Carhartt WIP，把熟悉外套接到另一种材料上

### 中文

11 月 13 日，T3 的 Matt Kollat 把 Nike × Carhartt WIP 的 Windrunner Active Jacket 放进本周选品。这个具体选择，让 11 月 12 日刚在 Carhartt WIP 渠道开售的系列有了一个比「又一次联名」更值得看的入口：两件大家认得的外套，怎样在结构上碰到一起。T3 的推荐带有 affiliate 链接，只代表编辑选择。[T3](https://www.t3.com/luxury/style/t3-style-edit-131126) · [Carhartt WIP](https://us.carhartt-wip.com/en-us/journal/fw26-carhartt-wip-x-nike)

Windrunner 原来围绕跑步活动设计。Nike 的档案把它放在 1979 年，记下轻质单层尼龙、插肩袖和 26 度 V 形。新夹克保留了 V 形，却换成石洗 Dearborn 棉帆布，加上袋鼠口袋、双向拉链与三重车线。Carhartt WIP 的另一篇档案则说明 Active Jacket 从连帽拉链运动衫长出加固外层。相交的位置已经能在衣服上指出来，不能顺手把原版跑步外套的性能承诺移过来。[Nike 档案](https://about.nike.com/en/magazine/5-decades-of-nike-innovation-in-women-s-apparel) · [夹克详情](https://www.carhartt-wip.com/en-sk/p/carhartt-wip-nike-windrunner-x-active-jacket-black-cypress-stone-washed-37) · [Progress Makes Icons](https://us.carhartt-wip.com/en-us/journal/fw26-delivery-i-icons)

2018 年那次合作，Sneaker News 记录的是 Air Force 1、Air Max 95 和 Vandal 等鞋型换上工装材料；Office 记录的宣传人物则有 Flohio、Shaniqwa Jarvis 和 Max Palmer。这次品牌用 35mm 短片《Nocturnes》跟随披萨师、篮球运动员与大提琴手穿行曼哈顿。创作者与日常路线，一直是品牌为这些物件搭的语境；广告里的日常不能代替穿着者访谈。[Sneaker News，2018](https://sneakernews.com/2018/11/19/carhartt-wip-nike-release-date/) · [Office，2018](https://officemagazine.net/nike-x-carhartt-wip)

去年，Wallpaper* 在 Active Jacket 五十周年装置中访问 ILL-STUDIO，讨论一件实用服装怎样积累使用者的意义。带着这条线索回看今天，联名最有意思的地方是熟悉结构被放进另一套材料，而意义还要在使用里慢慢形成。接下来可确认的时间点是 11 月 20 日鞋款进入 SNKRS 等渠道；服装仍由 Carhartt WIP 及其零售伙伴销售，不应把整个系列概括成同日登陆 Nike。[Wallpaper*，2025](https://www.wallpaper.com/fashion-beauty/carhartt-wip-50-years-active-jacket-tate-modern)

## U2 把新唱片交回四个人之间

### 中文

11 月 13 日，U2 发行了《Carnaval De Luz》。十二首歌由 Jacknife Lee 制作，这是乐队自 2017 年后第一张由全新材料组成的录音室专辑。官方同时放出一本 124 页的《Propaganda》特别刊，把制作人与影像合作者的访谈、歌词和成员笔记放在唱片旁边。它给这次回归留出了一个比单曲更慢的入口。[U2](https://www.u2.com/blogs/news/out-now-1)；[AP](https://apnews.com/article/november-2026-streaming-tv-music-movies-465085508c38bfcc487ed247d4b987f5)

这张唱片最值得追问的，是四个人重新怎样一起演奏。Pitchfork 把它读成成员关系的重新确认；Rolling Stone 西语版则把更集中、简明的制作，与此前分散的方向并读。两篇评论的共同观察落在演奏里，不能直接换算成一次商业复兴。[Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/u2-carnaval-de-luz/)；[Rolling Stone](https://es.rollingstone.com/arg-critica-el-sonido-de-u2-se-revitaliza-en-carnaval-de-luz/)

具体的支点仍然是歌。Louder 肯定《La Reina》和《Superficiality》的活力，《Irish Times》把 The Edge 的吉他放在中心。后者对一些歌词同样保留意见。制作变得清楚，与每一句歌词都站得住，是两件需要分别判断的事。[Louder](https://www.loudersound.com/bands-artists/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review)；[Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/music/review/2026/11/06/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review-irish-rocks-crazy-diamonds-shine-on-brighter-than-ever/)

Guardian 肯定回升的能量；LA Times 对歌词仍有保留。[Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/nov/09/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review)；[LA Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/music/story/2026-11-13/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review)

分歧把「回归」这个词拆得更具体。El País 喜欢恢复的乐器与人声表现，也批评西语发音和部分词句；《The Mirror Maker (Rosalía)》是受 Rosalía 启发的歌，不能误写成她参与的合唱。Aftonbladet 对整张作品是否构成有说服力的回归更加谨慎，承认亮点，也保留对身份与方向的疑问。[El País](https://elpais.com/cultura/2026-11-13/critica-de-carnaval-de-luz-de-u2-por-fin-otra-vez-inspirados-incluso-en-su-rendicion-a-rosalia.html)；[Aftonbladet](https://www.aftonbladet.se/nojesbladet/musik/a/3BjnzP/u2-carnaval-de-luz-recension)

把这些材料放在一起，我更愿意把它看成一次重新分配注意力的唱片。乐队仍然带着自己的语言，也暴露出这些语言的惯性。可以先听四件乐器如何互相接住，再判断那些宏大的句子是否获得了足够的重量。五十年的历史提供背景，歌曲仍需要自己完成说服。

### English

U2 released Carnaval De Luz on November 13. Produced by Jacknife Lee, its twelve songs form the band’s first studio album of new material since 2017. The release also includes a 124-page Propaganda special with lyrics, notes and interviews with the record’s creative collaborators. Together they offer a slower route into the project than a single alone. [U2](https://www.u2.com/blogs/news/out-now-1); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/november-2026-streaming-tv-music-movies-465085508c38bfcc487ed247d4b987f5)

A useful question is how the four members play together again. Pitchfork foregrounds their relationship; Rolling Stone’s Spanish-language review hears a more focused production identity. Those are observations about a record, rather than proof of a commercial revival. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/u2-carnaval-de-luz/); [Rolling Stone](https://es.rollingstone.com/arg-critica-el-sonido-de-u2-se-revitaliza-en-carnaval-de-luz/)

Louder praises the vitality of “La Reina” and “Superficiality.” The Irish Times emphasizes The Edge’s guitar while retaining reservations about some lyrics. A clearer ensemble sound does not settle every question about the writing. [Louder](https://www.loudersound.com/bands-artists/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review); [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/culture/music/review/2026/11/06/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review-irish-rocks-crazy-diamonds-shine-on-brighter-than-ever/)

The Guardian hears renewed energy; the Los Angeles Times retains lyrical reservations. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/nov/09/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review); [LA Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/music/story/2026-11-13/u2-carnaval-de-luz-review)

El País also praises the playing and singing while questioning pronunciation and lyrics. Its discussion clarifies that “The Mirror Maker (Rosalía)” is inspired by Rosalía, rather than a duet with her. Aftonbladet is more cautious about the comeback narrative as a whole. [El País](https://elpais.com/cultura/2026-11-13/critica-de-carnaval-de-luz-de-u2-por-fin-otra-vez-inspirados-incluso-en-su-rendicion-a-rosalia.html); [Aftonbladet](https://www.aftonbladet.se/nojesbladet/musik/a/3BjnzP/u2-carnaval-de-luz-recension)

Read together, the reviews suggest a record that redistributes attention toward the group’s interplay without escaping its familiar habits. Listen to how the instruments support one another, then decide whether the larger declarations have earned their weight. Fifty years supplies the context; the songs still have to persuade.

## Paris Photo最后一天：照片怎样成为物件、动作与界面
