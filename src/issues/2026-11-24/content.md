# 重新布置现场

Setting the Scene Again

雅典把老卫城博物馆重新打开，蒋琼耳在吉萨用十二尊雕塑排出一条步道，David Sims 则把三十五年的照片放进九个展厅。这一期沿着这些现场往下看，也收进东京快闪、抓绒联名、腰旗橄榄球和游戏里的电台。

## 雅典老卫城博物馆重开，修复也成了展览

### 中文

11月23日，雅典老卫城博物馆在闭馆19年后重新开放。馆史记载，这座博物馆于1865年设立、1874年建成，最初承接卫城考古发现；藏品在2007年迁往新馆后，老建筑转入新的等待期。To Vima回顾了它曾被讨论拆除、改作其他用途，之后又用于存放出土文物的过程。建筑嵌在帕特农神庙东南侧，设计刻意压低轮廓，让古迹仍居于视线中心。

这次重开把四种工作放回同一座建筑：考古展陈、数字展示、可见的修复工作，以及当代艺术。To Vima记述了西翼开放式考古藏品库和修复实验室；NEON的开幕信息列出并行开放的考古与数字项目；雅典文化指南Athinorama也把Rakowitz的展览放在同一周的新展清单中。

Rakowitz的新委托作品《What You Leave Behind Is Not What Is Engraved》是“Michael Rakowitz & Ancient Cultures”三部曲的第三部分，展期从2026年延续至2028年。NEON将作品描述为关于离散经验，以及不同语境中的物件如何生成新叙事；创作过程邀请市民、难民和侨民社群参与工作坊，以砖、陶土和印记构成作品。

Rakowitz过去的作品持续触及被毁或离散的文物与人。他把伊拉克失踪文物的档案转化为新的物质形态，并称这些作品是“重新显现”，而非复原原物；Art21记录了他对项目起点和家族迁徙记忆的解释。《The Art Newspaper》对2025年三部曲首展的报道、Frieze对作品中空缺与替代关系的评论，以及爱知三年展对其材料与展陈的介绍，都为理解这次新委托提供了不同角度。《纽约客》补充了其家族的伊拉克犹太背景；艺术家官网列出延续中的相关项目。

重开的老馆把考古保存、修复与当代创作并置，让“博物馆如何承接记忆”成为现场的一部分。新展把迁徙者留下的印记带进一座长期收藏古物的建筑；这两种记忆工作的并排呈现，为老馆的新角色提供了具体内容。

### English

Athens’s Old Acropolis Museum reopened on November 23 after 19 years closed. The museum’s history dates its founding to 1865 and the completion of its first building to 1874. After the collection moved to the newer museum in 2007, the old building entered a long period of uncertain use. To Vima recounts proposals to demolish or repurpose it, followed by its use for storing finds from the Acropolis. Built into the ground southeast of the Parthenon, the building was designed to keep its profile low beside the ancient monument.

The reopening brings four kinds of work into the same building: archaeological displays, digital interpretation, visible conservation work, and contemporary art. To Vima describes an open archaeological storage facility in the west wing and a conservation laboratory. NEON’s opening information lists the archaeological and digital projects running alongside Rakowitz’s exhibition, and Athens arts guide Athinorama includes the show in its roundup of the week’s openings.

Rakowitz’s new commission, *What You Leave Behind Is Not What Is Engraved*, is the third part of *Michael Rakowitz & Ancient Cultures* and is scheduled to run from 2026 through 2028. NEON frames it around diaspora and the new narratives objects can generate across contexts. The artist developed the work through workshops with citizens, refugees, and diaspora communities, using brick, clay, and imprints as materials for memory.

Rakowitz’s wider practice returns to displaced people and destroyed or missing objects. He turns records of missing Iraqi artifacts into new material forms, which he describes as “reappearances” rather than reconstructions. Art21 records his account of the project and its connection to family displacement. *The Art Newspaper* covered the first part of the trilogy in 2025. Frieze discusses absence and substitution in his work. Aichi Triennale documents how his materials and installations engage museum narratives. *The New Yorker* adds context about his Iraqi Jewish family background, while the artist’s site lists related ongoing projects.

My editorial reading is that the reopening makes the museum’s work of holding memory visible. Archaeological preservation, conservation, and contemporary art now share one setting; Rakowitz’s commission brings marks made by displaced communities into a building long associated with ancient finds. This is an interpretation of the programme’s arrangement, not a claim about audience response or social impact.

## 十二尊雕塑为吉萨排出一条步道

### 中文

第六届“Forever Is Now”将于11月28日在吉萨高原收官。蒋琼耳的《万物有时，此刻播种爱》把十二尊金色守护者排在一条通向金字塔的路径两侧。Wallpaper*报道，镜面底座和低矮镜墙映出沙地、天空与行走的人；观众可以沿路走近金字塔，再折返。作品延续了她在巴黎吉美国立亚洲艺术博物馆《Guardians of Time》里呈现的守护者系列，这一次则把它们放进一段可以用身体经过的现场关系。

这些形象汇集中国、埃及、玛雅和印度河文明的神话线索，各自对应和平、勇气、智慧等价值。主办方和蒋琼耳把这次作品与中埃建交70周年相连；新华社的现场报道记录了她对“寻找共同点”的解释。艺术家官网还介绍了另一层构成：她设计了一个新的“爱”字象形符号，并刻在12,135颗种子形木片上；Wallpaper*报道，她在开幕期间表演播撒这些木片。

镜面跟着观众的脚步改变。排开的雕塑把人引到金字塔前，手里的种子形木片又把视线拉回近处。A7杂志也把穿行和播种读作作品的关键动作。这里的“种下未来”是艺术家的象征表达，不是种子生长结果的证据；我更在意的是，观众在路径里从远看古迹，转为亲自经过它的边界。

### English

The sixth edition of Forever Is Now closes on the Giza Plateau on November 28. Jiang Qiong Er’s It Is Time to Plant Love places twelve golden guardians along a path leading towards the pyramids. Wallpaper* reports that mirrored bases and a low mirrored divider reflect the sand, sky and visitors; people can walk towards the pyramids and then return. The work extends the guardian series she presented at the Musée Guimet in Paris, while turning it here into an experience visitors move through.

The figures draw on mythologies from China, Egypt, Maya and the Indus Valley, and represent values such as peace, bravery and wisdom. Jiang and the organisers connect the project to the 70th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Egypt and China; Xinhua’s report records her explanation that she is looking for common ground. Her project page describes a new hieroglyph for “love” engraved on 12,135 wooden, seed-shaped pieces. Wallpaper* reports that she performed the act of scattering them during the opening.

My editorial reading is that the work does not answer the ancient site with another monumental object. It turns viewing into a walk, then brings the idea of “the future” down to a small seed-shaped wooden piece. A7 Magazine also reads walking and planting as the work’s key actions. “Planting the future” is a symbolic proposition, not evidence that seeds grew; what interests me is how the path changes the visitor’s role from looking at the monument from afar to passing through its frame.

## David Sims 把三十五年的照片放进九个房间

### 中文

11月21日，西班牙A Coruña的MOP Foundation开出David Sims摄影展《The Hamster Becomes The Problem》，展期至2027年5月1日。基金会把作品放在时尚与纪实、亲密与表演、反叛与温柔之间；到场不必预约，常规入场免费。策展人 Charlotte Cotton 让观众沿着不同房间自己拼接作品的关系。

《EL PAÍS》给出了展览的尺度。三十五年的工作、两百多张照片，分在九个各自布置的房间。入口、轮播装置和每个房间的陈列方式都在变，照片之间的关系由空间重新组织。《Vogue España》在 11 月 23 日的采访里又把几张作品带回拍摄的来历。先在展厅里找到彼此呼应的照片，再回去看它们当时为什么被拍下来，两种顺序都能读到不同的东西。

Sims 给策展人 Charlotte Cotton 的要求很短，少把注意力放在裙子上。展览仍包括他为品牌和杂志拍摄的作品，也呈现个人影像；MOP自己的说明把它放在时尚与纪实之间。更准确的读法是，衣服不再是唯一的入口。W Magazine写到，Sims在商业委托里也保留自己的手势；他在《The Business of Fashion》访谈中谈到，数字摄影让他能做非线性、陌生、甚至一度不确定会不会被刊出的图像。

展厅里有一只放大到人可以走进去的仓鼠轮，轮子转动时传来Iggy Pop的声音。当地《El Ideal Gallego》记录了Cotton给出的几种可能：仓鼠可以是持续工作几十年的Sims，也可以指向不断生产图像的劳作；报道没有替观众选出唯一答案。《L’Œil de la Photographie》把标题读成时尚消费循环的隐喻，那是评论者的解释，不是展览的标准答案。轮子因此不只是一个好拍的装置，它把回顾展的难题摆到面前：当图像不停被要求出现，创作者怎样不被自己的工作困住？

Sims与《The Face》、i-D等杂志的长期关系，也让作品一直活在编辑页的节奏里。System Magazine的访谈里，他谈到摄影可以保留个人提问，也可以拒绝一套固定的技术仪式；BoF访谈则记录他担心作品变成公式。展览把这些影像从杂志页抽出来，放进九个互不相同的空间；它没有替Sims总结出一个固定答案，而把摄影师如何继续看、继续做，留成现场的问题。BLIND Magazine对其早期编辑作品的回顾，以及Air Mail对展览类型和展期的记录，补上了这段档案的另一侧。

### English

On November 21, the MOP Foundation in A Coruña, Spain, opened David Sims’s exhibition *The Hamster Becomes The Problem*. It runs until May 1, 2027. The foundation places the work between fashion and documentary, intimacy and performance, rebellion and tenderness. General admission is free and does not require a booking. Instead of a chronological biography, the exhibition asks visitors to assemble the work through space.

El País puts the exhibition’s scale at more than two hundred photographs across thirty-five years of work, spread through nine rooms with distinct installations. The entrance, rotating displays and room layouts put the pictures in new relationships. A November 23 interview in Vogue España gives several of them their production context. The pictures invite one route through the rooms and another through the reasons they were made.

Sims gave curator Charlotte Cotton a brief in a few words: no dresses. That does not mean the galleries contain no fashion photographs. The show includes work made for brands and magazines alongside personal images; MOP describes it as moving between fashion and documentary. A more careful reading is that clothing is no longer the only entry point. *W Magazine* describes how Sims retained his own gestures within commissioned work. In an interview with *The Business of Fashion*, he says digital photography let him make nonlinear, unfamiliar images that he was not sure would be published.

One gallery contains a human-scale hamster wheel, with Iggy Pop’s voice playing as it turns. Local newspaper *El Ideal Gallego* records several possibilities offered by Cotton: the hamster could be Sims, working for decades, or it could point to the labor of producing images without end. The report does not choose one answer for visitors. *L’Œil de la Photographie* reads the title as a metaphor for fashion’s consumption cycle; that is the critic’s interpretation, not an official key. The wheel is more than a photogenic prop: it puts a question about the retrospective in front of us. When images are constantly demanded, how does a maker avoid being trapped by the work?

Sims’s long relationship with magazines such as *The Face* and *i-D* also means that much of his work has lived at the pace of the editorial page. In an interview with *System Magazine*, he discusses keeping a personal line of inquiry in his photography rather than following one fixed technical ritual; BoF reports that he worries about work becoming formulaic. The exhibition moves those images off the magazine page and into nine distinct spaces. It does not settle on one fixed answer about Sims, but leaves the question of how a photographer keeps looking and making work open for the visitor. *BLIND Magazine* revisits his early editorial work, while *Air Mail* documents the exhibition’s format and run.

## 威尼斯双年展闭幕，Koyo Kouoh 留下的展览怎样完成

### 中文

11月22日闭幕的威尼斯双年展，官方公布有714,015名观众和100个国家参与；本届比往届短三周。这个数字说明它吸引了多少人走进场馆，不等于对展览质量的一次投票，更不能替一位已经离世的策展人完成最后评语。

主展“In Minor Keys”由Koyo Kouoh构想。她在2025年去世后，五位长期与她合作的策展同事继续完成展览。官方材料记载，Kouoh已经确定了理论框架、艺术家与作品、图录作者、视觉识别和展场设计；《艺术报》进一步报道，团队在她离世前数月就共同推进主题、布展与选件。这不是临时找人替她交稿，也不能因此推定每个现场决定都等同于她本人会作出的选择。

评论也没有汇成一个声音。《艺术报》给主展三星半，称作品有力、整体却不均衡；《布鲁克林铁路》赞赏Giardini展厅里作品之间的联系，却觉得Arsenale部分有些仓促；当代艺术协会的评论把作品并置理解成一种扰动既有分类的方法，同时也指出观众需要自己承担大量梳理工作。Afterall则把这种游移读成“听见不合拍之处”的观看方法。作品、空间和观众之间的关系，仍留着不同读法。

奖项的变化把另一层分歧带到了现场。国际评审团于 4 月辞职后，主办方设置了两座「观众狮」。根据官方规则，持票观众需要到访 Giardini 和 Arsenale 两处展区才能投票。展览怎样布置、评委依据什么给奖、观众实际走完哪条路线，原本就是三个问题。观众狮只改动了最后那一票由谁投。

闭幕回执能确认规模、团队完成的工作和评审机制的变化；它不能证明这就是Kouoh会亲自完成的最终形态，也不能用714,015这个数字把评论分歧盖过去。双年展把策展人留下的方向继续带到了公共空间，关于它是否成功地让这些“低音”被听见，答案仍散落在作品与观众各自的经验里。

### English

The Venice Biennale closed on 22 November with 714,015 visitors and 100 national participations, according to the organizers. This edition ran three weeks shorter than previous ones. The figure shows how many people entered the venues; it is not a vote on the exhibition’s quality, and it cannot deliver a final judgment on behalf of a curator who is no longer here.

Koyo Kouoh conceived the main exhibition, “In Minor Keys.” After her death in 2025, five curatorial colleagues who had worked with her over time carried it through. The Biennale says Kouoh had established the theoretical framework, selected artists and works, chosen catalogue contributors, and set the graphic identity and exhibition design. The Art Newspaper reports that the team had already worked with her for months on the themes, scenography and selection. This was not a last-minute replacement assignment, but that does not mean every decision in the finished exhibition was one she would have made herself.

The reviews did not settle into one voice. The Art Newspaper gave the main show three and a half stars, finding strong work but an uneven whole. The Brooklyn Rail praised connections among works in the Giardini, while finding parts of the Arsenale rushed. The Contemporary Arts Society read the juxtapositions as a way to unsettle existing categories, while also noting how much work visitors had to do to orient themselves. Afterall read that movement as a way of attending to what is out of tune. The relationships between works, space and visitors remain open to different readings.

The awards brought a different dispute into the exhibition. After the international jury resigned in April, the organizers established two Visitors’ Lions. The official rules required ticket holders to have visited both the Giardini and the Arsenale to vote. Curating the exhibition, judging an award and making a route through the venues are different jobs. The new award changed who cast the vote.

The closing record confirms the scale, the team’s work and the change in how prizes were handled. It cannot prove that this was the final form Kouoh herself would have made, or use 714,015 visitors to settle the critical disagreements. The Biennale carried her direction into public space; whether it made these “minor keys” audible remains dispersed across the works and each visitor’s experience.

## Beau Disundi 用咸鱼纸箱画贸易

### 中文

11月19日，达喀尔当代非洲艺术双年展把本届最高奖授予刚果（金）艺术家 Beau Disundi，奖金为2000万西非法郎。官方介绍把他的创作概括为追问历史、经济、货物流通与全球化的文化影响，并特别提到一种寻常食材：鳕鱼。

Disundi 把干制咸鳕鱼 makayabu 的包装纸箱当作创作材料。La Cambre 的校友档案把鳕鱼列为贯穿其创作的线索，AKKA Project 的作品页中也能找到 makayabu 纸板、粉彩和金属。纸箱留着一件日常商品经过的路径，点出这段贸易如何进入人们的厨房和生活。

这条贸易线并非只存在于作品的比喻里。2023年，刚果（金）通讯社 ACP 报道，刚果机构与挪威代表团曾讨论 makayabu ambassade 的进口和流通路径；挪威海产委员会2024年的报道则从出口方角度记下了两国围绕市场准入、进口条件的交流。两份材料各自呈现贸易链的一侧，不足以替代完整的进口统计，也不证明艺术作品对现实贸易产生了影响。

这次获奖把一个生活材料带进国际展览的评审视野，但不等于作品已经改变市场或机构版图。艺术评论家 Yacouba Konaté 在双年展前的访谈里，把 Dak’Art 的作用说成“认可”，而不是替艺术家规定方向；这给奖项提供了一个更克制的读法：它确认了谁的实践被看见，但接下来能否转化为展览、收藏或收入，现有材料还不能回答。Disundi 的个人页列出他在本届国际展中的位置；主展在达喀尔旧法院展出至12月19日，IN展免费开放。

### English

On November 19, the Dakar Biennale of Contemporary African Art awarded its Grand Prize to Congolese artist Beau Disundi. The prize carries 20 million CFA francs. The Biennale’s announcement describes his practice through its interest in history, economics, the circulation of goods and globalization’s cultural effects, with particular attention to an ordinary food: cod.

Disundi works on the cardboard used to package makayabu, dried salted cod. La Cambre’s alumni profile identifies cod as a thread in his practice. AKKA Project’s listings show how he uses the packaging with pastel and metal. The surface of a painting thus carries a trace of a commodity that travels into people’s kitchens.

That trade route is not only a metaphor in the work. In 2023, the Congolese news agency ACP reported discussions between Congolese institutions and a Norwegian delegation about importing and distributing makayabu ambassade. A 2024 report by the Norwegian Seafood Council records talks about market access and import conditions from the exporters’ perspective. These sources show two sides of a trade relationship; they do not amount to complete import statistics or show that the artwork changed commercial practice.

The prize places a material from everyday life before an international exhibition jury, but it does not show that the work has already changed the market or the institutions around it. In a pre-opening interview, art critic Yacouba Konaté described Dak’Art’s role as “validation,” not prescription. That offers a more measured reading of the award: it confirms whose practice is being seen, while the available evidence cannot yet say whether that recognition will lead to further exhibitions, collections or income. Disundi’s Biennale profile lists him in the international exhibition. The main show is at Dakar’s former courthouse through December 19, and the IN exhibitions are free.

## Willi Smith 把衣服摆在画廊地上

### 中文

时装进美术馆，常被摆成回顾展；MoMA PS1这次把问题往前推了一步：1980年代初，衣服能不能直接成为展览的方法？《New York Fashion at PS1, 1980–1984》正在纽约皇后区展出，回看独立策展人 Hollywood Di Russo 与纽约设计师合作的项目。PS1把时装放进一个同时容纳声音、表演、诗歌、电影和建筑的多学科计划里；1980至1984年间共办了十场时装展。

1982 年，Willi Smith 把服装浸入白色石膏，排在画廊地面。MoMA 的历史档案保留了现场照。今天的展览用旧 Butterick 纸样和重新制作的衣服，局部重建了那件装置。《W Magazine》的报道还记录了 Julio、Homer Layne 和 Betsey Johnson 等人的作品如何与档案、视频放在一起。

旧展留下的信件、照片、邀请函和影片，让今天的策展人能拼出一部分陈列。MoMA 档案员回顾 1981 年的展览时，记下 Homer Layne 与 Di Russo 一起展出了 Charles James 的 26 件礼服和其他服装，还陈列了原始绘图。现在再看这些线索，能想象一所旧校舍如何被拿来做时装实验。

衣服躺在地上和穿在身上，给观众的是两种完全不同的观察位置。艺术家用服装的结构回应房间，美术馆用旧照片和今天的局部重建让我们看到当时的做法。展览在 MoMA PS1 持续到 2027 年 3 月 15 日，免费入场。

### English

Fashion exhibitions often take the form of retrospectives. MoMA PS1’s *New York Fashion at PS1, 1980–1984* asks an earlier question: could clothing itself become a way of making an exhibition? Open in Queens through March 15, 2027, the show revisits projects developed by independent curator Hollywood Di Russo with New York designers. PS1 placed fashion inside a multidisciplinary program that also encompassed sound, performance, poetry, film and architecture; ten fashion exhibitions took place from 1980 to 1984.

In 1982 Willi Smith dipped garments in white plaster and laid them out on the gallery floor. MoMA’s archival record preserves a view of that installation. The current exhibition uses vintage Butterick patterns and remade garments to reconstruct part of it. W Magazine also describes how archival material and film accompany projects by Julio, Homer Layne and Betsey Johnson.

Letters, photographs, invitations and film help the curators recover parts of those early displays. A MoMA archivist records that in 1981 Homer Layne and Di Russo showed twenty-six Charles James gowns alongside other garments and original drawings. The surviving material reveals how a former school building became a place to test what a fashion exhibition could be.

Clothes laid out on the floor ask you to look from a different position than clothes worn on a body. The original installations used garments to respond to rooms. MFA公布的12个“时代”提供了一条清楚的时间线，馆方也说展览会呈现歌曲创作、表演与视觉叙事的结合。但现在看到的是展览计划，展签如何讲服装、器乐、创作和制作团队，仍要等到开幕才知道。判断它是否只把粉丝熟悉的造型陈列出来，或能让观众看到这些作品怎样被制作、传播和记住，还需要看现场。展览尚未开门，争论可以先开始，结论应当晚一点。

### English

The Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, announced *Taylor Swift: The Eras Collection* for 10 April to 15 August 2027. It will bring together more than 50 costumes, instruments and archival objects, arranged across 12 career “eras,” from “Tim McGraw” (2006) to “The Fate of Ophelia” (2025). It is the first exhibition devoted to a musician in the MFA’s history, and Boston will be its only venue. It is not the first museum exhibition to show Swift’s objects: the GRAMMY Museum, the Museum of Arts and Design in New York and the Arlington Museum of Art have all presented earlier shows.

Those precedents took different approaches. In 2014 the GRAMMY Museum brought together items from Swift’s personal collection, handwritten lyrics, stage instruments and objects made by fans, alongside interactive music activities. MAD’s 2023 *Storyteller* connected costumes, props, jewellery and music-video projections to songwriting. Arlington’s exhibition selected eight original costumes from four periods. Clothing can be an entry point, but a garment cannot explain by itself how songs, stages, design labour and audiences relate.

The exhibition was announced after staffing and financial changes at the MFA. In January 2026, the museum said it would eliminate thirty-three existing jobs. The Boston Globe reported a projected $13 million shortfall at the time. The MFA’s FY2025 report says attendance returned to 1.05 million even as it ran an operating deficit. That makes both attendance and the substance of a visit worth watching when the exhibition opens in April.

The important question, then, is not whether pop music belongs in an art museum. The V&A’s David Bowie exhibition brought together costumes, film, stage design, sketches and diaries, and later toured to more than a million visitors worldwide. The V&A’s later account of Bowie’s archive also points out that an archive can reveal creative process without revealing everything about the person behind the work. The route of interpretation matters more than whether a celebrity is famous enough.

The MFA’s 12 “eras” offer a clear timeline, and the museum says the exhibition will show how songwriting, performance and visual storytelling work together. What we have now is an exhibition plan. How the labels connect clothing, instruments, creative work and the production teams behind it will not be clear until the show opens. To judge whether it merely displays familiar looks or helps visitors see how the work was made, circulated and remembered, we need to see the galleries. The exhibition is not open yet; the debate can start now, but the verdict can wait.
