# 空间如何随着人移动

SPACES IN MOTION

舞台上的桥随舞者移动，树皮布在赠与中延续关系，一张轻椅在户外与服装之间改变位置。今天从伦敦、迪拜和釜山出发，也去看音乐、衣服、岛屿与一座未来城市。

已经结束的活动和仍在计划中的安排各有日期。我们把现场报道、历史作品、设计设想和当下能够确认的事实分开，留出继续观察的空间。

## 扎哈的舞台，为什么总在移动？

### 中文

舞者推动三座桥，在桥上和桥下穿行，舞台的空间也随之改变。这是编舞家 Frédéric Flamand 与扎哈·哈迪德合作的《Metapolis》：一场 2000 年的舞蹈作品，让建筑和身体一起参与表演。 [Oriente Occidente](https://www.orienteoccidente.it/people/eventi/metapolis-project-972) · [El País](https://elpais.com/diario/2001/05/27/espectaculos/990914403_850215.html)

伦敦的扎哈·哈迪德基金会计划于当地时间 11 月 3 日向公众开放旧工作室，并推出《Architecture of Performance》。展览把 25 年里的五个项目放在一起：从编舞家 Rosemary Butcher 的《d1.》，到为 Pet Shop Boys、舞蹈作品《Metapolis》和歌剧《Così fan tutte》设计的空间。模型、图纸、服装与影像会共同出现。这些是媒体报道的展览安排，并不代表我们已经看过现场。 [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/architecture-events/zaha-hadid-foundation-new-space-and-events) · [Archie Interiors](https://www.archieinteriors.com/en/zaha-hadid-london-studio-cultural-centre/)

这条线索改变了看待扎哈的一种顺序。先不要把舞台当成著名建筑师偶尔设计的小物件，试着看人怎样穿过它。编舞家 Butcher 后来回忆，在 1989 年的《d1.》中，扎哈用胶带在地面画出空间线条，她再在其上叠加动作网格；次年的 Music Video Pavilion 则把影像投射到玻璃和地面，与观众在荷兰格罗宁根狭窄场地里的行走路线重叠。建筑尚未成为永久建筑，空间已经在被使用。 [Tanz im August](https://www.tanzimaugust.de/fileadmin/TiA/Archiv_Festival/2015/TiA_Magazin_2015_Webansicht_Einzelseiten_RGB.pdf) · [Hidden Architecture](https://hiddenarchitecture.net/music-video-pavilion/)

《Metapolis》把这个问题交给舞者。扎哈事务所的项目记录描述了可重新排列的半透明桥，以及能延伸动作的服装；《卫报》当年的演出评论观察到，桥体改变了舞蹈的空间形状。西班牙《国家报》对 2001 年马德里演出的评论，则更肯定建筑、舞蹈与实时影像之间的配合。《卫报》也提出保留：技术与影像的密度有时会压过人的存在。这个反对意见值得留在作品旁边，移动的空间不自动等于更好的表演。2006 年，Flamand 与马赛国家芭蕾舞团重演为《Metapolis II》，沿用原来的舞台设计，并让更多舞者进入其中。 [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2001/aug/27/dance.edinburghfestival2001) · [El País](https://elpais.com/diario/2001/05/27/espectaculos/990914403_850215.html) · [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2007-jul-25-et-metapolis25-story.html)

基金会将展览放在伦敦 Clerkenwell 的旧办公室。Wallpaper* 与 Archie Interiors 均报道，原有校舍经 Studio KA 更新后，将提供画廊、学习空间和讲座厅。对读者来说，重要的不只是旧建筑重新开放：它把原本分散在建筑、舞蹈和流行音乐里的工作放到同一张桌面上，给人机会重新观察空间如何随着身体、画面和时间变化。 [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/architecture-events/zaha-hadid-foundation-new-space-and-events) · [Archie Interiors](https://www.archieinteriors.com/en/zaha-hadid-london-studio-cultural-centre/)

### English

Dancers push three bridges, moving above and below them as the stage changes around them. In *Metapolis*, a 2000 work by choreographer Frédéric Flamand with a set by Zaha Hadid, architecture and moving bodies take part in the same performance. [Oriente Occidente](https://www.orienteoccidente.it/people/eventi/metapolis-project-972) · [El País](https://elpais.com/diario/2001/05/27/espectaculos/990914403_850215.html)

The Zaha Hadid Foundation plans to reopen the architect’s former London studio to the public on 3 November, local time, with *Architecture of Performance*. The exhibition brings together five projects across 25 years, from Rosemary Butcher’s dance work *d1.* to designs for the Pet Shop Boys, *Metapolis* and the opera *Così fan tutte*. Drawings, models, costumes and film will be shown together. These are the announced plans; we have not visited the exhibition. [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/architecture-events/zaha-hadid-foundation-new-space-and-events) · [Archie Interiors](https://www.archieinteriors.com/en/zaha-hadid-london-studio-cultural-centre/)

The sequence offers another way into Hadid’s work: follow the people moving through a space. Butcher later recalled that, in *d1.*, Hadid used tape to draw spatial lines on the floor, over which the choreographer placed a grid of movement. The 1990 Music Video Pavilion projected images onto glass and floor, intersecting the visitors’ route through a narrow site in Groningen. Ideas about space could be tested before they became permanent buildings. [Tanz im August](https://www.tanzimaugust.de/fileadmin/TiA/Archiv_Festival/2015/TiA_Magazin_2015_Webansicht_Einzelseiten_RGB.pdf) · [Hidden Architecture](https://hiddenarchitecture.net/music-video-pavilion/)

*Metapolis* handed the question to dancers. The design record describes movable translucent bridges and costumes that extended the performers’ gestures. A contemporary Guardian review noted how the structures changed the shape of the dance, while questioning whether the density of technology sometimes overwhelmed the human presence. A 2001 El País review of the Madrid performance was more positive about the interaction of architecture, dance and live video. A moving set does not automatically make a better performance. In 2006, Flamand recreated the piece as *Metapolis II* with the Ballet National de Marseille, retaining the original set and bringing in more dancers. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2001/aug/27/dance.edinburghfestival2001) · [El País](https://elpais.com/diario/2001/05/27/espectaculos/990914403_850215.html) · [Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2007-jul-25-et-metapolis25-story.html)

The exhibition is housed in the foundation’s former Clerkenwell office. Wallpaper* and Archie Interiors report that Studio KA has adapted the old school building to include galleries, learning space and a lecture hall. Bringing architecture, dance and pop together there makes room to examine how space changes with bodies, images and time. [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/architecture-events/zaha-hadid-foundation-new-space-and-events) · [Archie Interiors](https://www.archieinteriors.com/en/zaha-hadid-london-studio-cultural-centre/)

## 安全别针回到衣服上，Judy Blame的物件如何继续被使用

### 中文

11 月 2 日，uniform experiment 与 Trust Judy Blame 合作推出一组服装，纪念东京 SOPH. 门店 27 周年。品牌公告把安全别针，以及 Judy Blame 用泰晤士河边拾得物制作的首饰，转成夹克、衬衫和针织上的图像。这里进入衣服的是作品的视觉元素；不能因此说每件衣服本身都是由回收物制成。 [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html)

Blame 曾在采访里说，自己开始制作首饰是为了每周去夜店时更换造型。链条、骨头、旧硬币和纽扣都可能被他重新组合。在 2016 年的展览采访中，他还介绍过一条用马桶冲水拉链做成的项链。这些零件原本属于不同的生活，它们在身上重新变成一件东西。 [Interview Magazine](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/fashion/judy-blame) · [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html) · [AnOther](https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/8813/judy-blames-guided-tour-of-1980s-counterculture)

这次系列将那种组合转成了印花和服装。SOPH. 的公告列出飞行夹克、教练夹克、针织衫、卫衣与徽章等款式；合作机构 Trust Judy Blame 负责保存与传播他的作品。两者共同参与，说明新产品与档案机构存在公开合作关系；公开资料没有说明每件作品的具体使用安排或收益分配。 [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html)

Blame 的实践不止于首饰。AnOther 的采访回顾了他与 Ray Petri 的 Buffalo 团队，以及音乐和时尚造型工作。2016 年，伦敦 ICA 为他举办个展；他对 W Magazine 谈到，展览之外还做了一本小志，想让观众可以带走一些材料。时尚编辑 Terry Jones 后来回忆，他不断改造那些别人会丢掉的物件。 [AnOther](https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/2842/judy-blame) · [W Magazine](https://www.wmagazine.com/story/judy-blame-ica-exhibit-zine-interview) · [i-D](https://i-d.co/article/a-tribute-to-judy-blame-by-kim-jones-neneh-cherry-jeurgen-teller-mark-lebon/)

把别针印在新衣服上，和在河边捡起物件、亲手把它们连在一起，是两种不同的制作。前者让更多人能接触这个视觉档案；后者保留了材料原本的偶然性与手工判断。两者之间的距离，正是这次合作值得读的地方。

### English

On 2 November, uniform experiment released a clothing capsule with Trust Judy Blame for the 27th anniversary of SOPH. TOKYO. The brand’s announcement describes graphics drawn from safety pins and jewellery Blame made using objects found along the Thames. The collection transfers visual elements onto garments; it does not establish that the garments themselves are made from recovered objects. [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html)

Blame once said he began making jewellery to change his appearance for weekly nights out. Chains, bones, old coins and buttons could become part of a new composition. In a 2016 exhibition interview he also described a necklace made from a toilet-flush chain. Pieces with different earlier lives were assembled into something worn on the body. [Interview Magazine](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/fashion/judy-blame) · [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html) · [AnOther](https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/8813/judy-blames-guided-tour-of-1980s-counterculture)

The collection translates that approach into prints and garments. SOPH.’s announcement lists a flight jacket, coach jacket, knitwear, sweatshirts and badges among the pieces. Trust Judy Blame works to preserve and share his archive. Its involvement establishes a stated collaboration with the archive, while the public information does not explain the rights arrangement or how proceeds are distributed. [SOPH. / PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000004.000138303.html)

Blame’s work extended beyond jewellery. AnOther’s interview traces his connection with Ray Petri’s Buffalo collective and his work across music and fashion imagery. In 2016 London’s ICA presented a solo exhibition; he told W Magazine that he also made a small zine for visitors to take away. Editor Terry Jones later recalled Blame’s habit of reworking objects others might discard. [AnOther](https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/2842/judy-blame) · [W Magazine](https://www.wmagazine.com/story/judy-blame-ica-exhibit-zine-interview) · [i-D](https://i-d.co/article/a-tribute-to-judy-blame-by-kim-jones-neneh-cherry-jeurgen-teller-mark-lebon/)

Printing a safety pin on a new garment and finding an object by the river and joining it by hand are different acts. The former gives more people access to the archive’s imagery; the latter preserves the accident of the material and the maker’s physical decisions. The distance between them is part of what makes the collaboration worth examining.

## Yaeji的新歌，把跳舞时照顾彼此写进舞池

### 中文

Yaeji 在 11 月 2 日发行新单曲《ddr》。Bandcamp 页面记录曲长 4 分 37 秒，由 XL Recordings 发行。Pitchfork 报道，这首歌与纽约音乐人 Enayet、K Wata 合作，影像由 Giseong Jun 执导。标题指向跳舞机文化，歌曲和影像怎样回应它，还需要从实际观看与聆听中判断；这里先记录已公开的信息。 [Yaeji / XL Recordings](https://kraejiyaeji.bandcamp.com/album/ddr) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

她对这次发行的解释里，有一个比口号更具体的细节：共同跳舞时有人准备水，有人留意并阻止骚扰。Pitchfork 引述了她写给这首歌的文字。那是创作者想象中的舞池关系，不是对某个场地已经安全、所有参与者都得到照顾的证明。 [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

11 月 6 日，Yaeji 计划在布鲁克林 Paragon 举办以跳舞机为线索的夜间活动，节目包括她本人以及 DJ Swisha、OSSX、Lil C、DJ Noir 的演出。[Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

把新歌放回她自己的路径里，照顾与跳舞并非突然出现的两个词。2017 年，Yaeji 对《卫报》说，她曾在与朋友共同演出的活动上供应咖喱，给跳舞的人补充体力。次年她对 The FADER 解释，自己也希望把演出机会分享给此前较少被看见的音乐人。2021 年，Yaeji 在 Pitchfork 的对谈里谈到停摆期间重新思考自己的音乐意图，也开始更认真地理解音乐与历史的关系。2023 年 Pitchfork 的采访还记录，她把自己的工作室提供给同行共同工作与学习，费用按各自能力分担。2024 年她接受东京演出场地采访时说，休息一段时间后，自己又重新享受去俱乐部和分享音乐。这些采访发生在不同处境中，不必把它们解释成一条预设好的成长故事。 [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/dec/08/house-producer-yaeji-pretty-shy-about-using-my-voice) · [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2018/06/05/yaeji-cover-story) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/features/cover-story/yaeji-amaarae-bartees-strange-keiyaa-angel-bat-dawid-interview/) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/features/profile/yaeji-with-a-hammer-interview/) · [Spotify O-EAST / K-MUSIC](https://shibuya-o.com/feature/yaeji-interview/)

《ddr》目前作为单曲发行。跳舞机给了它一个可见的动作，而水、彼此留意和共同在场，则是 Yaeji 为这次作品提出的另一层邀请。它是否能在 11 月 6 日的活动里变成真实的现场经验，要等活动发生后再说。

### English

Yaeji released the single *ddr* on 2 November. Her Bandcamp page lists a running time of 4 minutes 37 seconds and identifies XL Recordings as the label. Pitchfork reports that the track was made with New York collaborators Enayet and K Wata, with a video directed by Giseong Jun. Its title invokes dance arcade culture; any judgment about how the music and film use that reference requires listening and viewing. [Yaeji / XL Recordings](https://kraejiyaeji.bandcamp.com/album/ddr) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

In her explanation of the release, Yaeji describes people making sure there is water and looking out for harassment on a shared dancefloor, as quoted by Pitchfork. This is the artist’s idea of what that space could be, not proof that a particular venue is safe or that every participant has been cared for. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

On 6 November she plans to host a dance-game party at Paragon in Brooklyn, with DJ sets by Yaeji, DJ Swisha, OSSX, Lil C and DJ Noir. [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/story/yaeji-plots-a-dance-dance-revolution-in-new-ddr-video/)

In 2017, Yaeji told the Guardian that she had served curry at shows with friends, giving people who danced through the night something to eat. The next year she told The FADER that she wanted to share performance opportunities with musicians who had received less attention. In a 2021 Pitchfork conversation, Yaeji described reconsidering her intentions and studying the relationship between music and history during the pandemic. A 2023 Pitchfork interview also described her opening her studio to peers for work and learning, with contributions toward costs based on ability. In a 2024 interview with a Tokyo venue, she said time away had helped her enjoy clubbing and sharing music again. These interviews come from different moments and do not need to be turned into a predetermined story of progress. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/dec/08/house-producer-yaeji-pretty-shy-about-using-my-voice) · [The FADER](https://www.thefader.com/2018/06/05/yaeji-cover-story) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/features/cover-story/yaeji-amaarae-bartees-strange-keiyaa-angel-bat-dawid-interview/) · [Pitchfork](https://pitchfork.com/features/profile/yaeji-with-a-hammer-interview/) · [Spotify O-EAST / K-MUSIC](https://shibuya-o.com/feature/yaeji-interview/)

For now, *ddr* is a released single and the party is still to come. The arcade game offers an image of movement; the artist’s attention to water and looking out for one another adds another possible way to think about being together on a dancefloor.

## 一座花园、一种文字：迪拜设计周如何安排相遇

### 中文

今年的迪拜设计周，有一个作品从阿拉伯字母 ش 出发。设计师 Dalia Rafeh 把与“安静”相关的发声转成一道红光，用公共招牌的语言，让人注意到一次停顿。它是 Abwab 群展《In Other Words》中的一件作品。 [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/art-design/2026/10/22/dubai-design-week-announces-five-new-commissions-for-abwab-2026/)

第十二届 Dubai Design Week 计划于 11 月 3 日至 8 日在 Dubai Design District 举行；单独的 Downtown Design 博览会为 11 月 4 日至 8 日。Abwab 今年由过去的独立展亭形式转为室内群展，五组创作者共享一个空间。上面的作品说明来自主办方的项目资料，展览是否实现了预想的观看体验，还需要现场判断。 [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/art-design/2026/10/13/dubai-design-week-2026-announces-programme-spanning-architecture-and-craft/)

群展中，VARUSSI 从克什米尔地毯与披肩的织造记谱出发，将设计师与织工之间传递指令的系统转译成空间；Grace Shieh 则以手工纸、诗歌和实验写作讨论记忆如何留在纤维与纸面。The Gulf Pulse 报道，《Taleem》获得本地室内设计公司 Faham 的材料支持，方案使用回收木材，并计划在展后拆卸再组装；这仍是展前描述。当地工艺组织 Dastkari Haat Samiti 记录过另一件 Kani 披肩的制作：织工把传递颜色与数量的记谱本身织成纹样。《Kashmir Life》采访的设计师则解释，他先在方格纸上画出图案，再转成颜色符号。印度工艺与设计学院记录的一次工作坊还展示了织工如何把彩色纬线缠在小木梭上，再按记谱织入经线。这些是展览以外的工艺背景，帮助理解指令如何在不同人的手中流转。两件作品处理的材料很不同，问题却有交集：知识不一定只靠完整句子保存，它也可能藏在制作和传递的方法里。 [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/art-design/2026/10/22/dubai-design-week-announces-five-new-commissions-for-abwab-2026/) · [The Gulf Pulse](https://thegulfpulse.com/dubai-design-week-2026-taleem/) · [Dastkari Haat Samiti](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/xgWR-XyyUK4VIg) · [Kashmir Life](https://kashmirlife.net/kanihama-the-loom-village-vol-15-issue-07-317356/) · [Indian Institute of Crafts and Design](https://www.iicd.ac.in/kani-weaving-craft/)

## 塔什干的新城模型，如何走向公共视野？

### 中文

塔什干设计周按已公布的日程于 11 月 1 日结束。活动原计划在 Tashkent City Mall 首次向公众展示新塔什干模型。我们核实了设计师的现场发言，但暂未找到模型实际展出的独立现场记录。2025 年，Kun.uz 曾在另一座模型旁采访规划负责人；那次报道不能证明今年的公开展示已经发生。模型计划呈现住宅、道路和公共空间的安排，也留下一个更难回答的问题：未来居民如何使用这座城市？ [Tafs.uz](https://tafs.uz/en/2026/10/21/new-tashkent-model-to-be-publicly-unveiled-for-the-first-time-at-tashkent-design-week-2026) · [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/ru/news/2025/06/07/kakim-budet-novyy-tashkent-intervyu-s-glavnym-arxitektorom-i-gradostroitelem-proyekta)

新塔什干位于现有首都以东。乌兹别克斯坦总统网站公布的规划范围为 19,700 公顷，首期计划为 60 万人的生活和工作提供基础设施；全部完成后按 200 万人规划。这些是目标，不是现有人口。10 月 12 日的官方消息称，首期七项设施已经启用，包括学校、住宅项目的一期及道路设施。 [President of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://www.president.uz/en/lists/view/8845) · [President of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://www.president.uz/en/lists/view/9707)

在设计周的公开讨论中，总规划设计方 Cross Works 的 Hakan Ceyhan Agca 谈到河道、不同等级的道路和计划中的轨道交通；Gazeta.uz 的报道也记录了约 28% 用地拟留作开放空间。这里的河道、交通与空间比例都是规划者的设想，尚不能证明它们将如何运行。 [Gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/10/28/new-tashkent/)

设计周的节目还包括家具、工艺与建筑项目。Afisha.uz 的报道提到，设计师 Zulfizara 的物件借鉴当地建筑中的拱廊和图案；另一个名为 Mahalla Urban Living 的装置则以社区空间为灵感。这些项目与新城模型被安排在同一个节目里，却处在很不同的尺度上：一个是可搬动的物件，一个是生活场所的设想。 [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/gorod/2026/10/28/tashkent-design-week)

今年，现有塔什干的十处现代主义建筑和城市综合体进入联合国教科文组织《世界遗产名录》。曼彻斯特大学研究者 Garrett Wolf 在实地研究中分析过其中的珍珠住宅楼：楼内每三层设有共享庭院，借用了 mahalla 的空间经验。他还记录了居民与物业如何在不同时期维护和改变公共空间。这是对现有建筑的研究，不能据此断言新城也会出现相同结果。 [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/public-buildings/unesco-world-heritage-list-modernist-additions) · [University of Manchester](https://pure.manchester.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/216118310/FULL_TEXT.PDF)

活动日程已到结束日期，但我们尚未核实模型实际展示的情况；居民对未来通勤、服务和公共空间的评价，仍需要继续采访和现场观察。住房、交通、公共服务与环境如何配合，是比模型外观更具体的后续观察方向。

### English

Tashkent Design Week was scheduled to conclude on 1 November. The programme planned a first public display of a New Tashkent model at Tashkent City Mall. We verified the designer’s talk but have not found an independent on-site account confirming the model was displayed. In 2025, Kun.uz interviewed planning officials beside another model; that earlier report does not confirm the public display planned for 2026. The planned model would make housing, roads and public spaces visible together, while leaving a harder question: how will people use the city? [Tafs.uz](https://tafs.uz/en/2026/10/21/new-tashkent-model-to-be-publicly-unveiled-for-the-first-time-at-tashkent-design-week-2026) · [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/ru/news/2025/06/07/kakim-budet-novyy-tashkent-intervyu-s-glavnym-arxitektorom-i-gradostroitelem-proyekta)

New Tashkent is planned to the east of the existing capital. The Uzbek president’s office gives a planned area of 19,700 hectares and says the first phase is intended to provide infrastructure for 600,000 people to live and work. The completed project is planned for two million residents. These are targets, not the present population. An official update on 12 October reported that seven first-phase facilities had been commissioned, including a school, a phase of a residential complex and road infrastructure. [President of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://www.president.uz/en/lists/view/8845) · [President of the Republic of Uzbekistan](https://www.president.uz/en/lists/view/9707)

At a design-week talk, Hakan Ceyhan Agca of master-planning firm Cross Works discussed canals, different classes of roads and planned rail transport. Local publication Gazeta.uz also recorded a proposed allocation of about 28 per cent of the area to open space. The canals, transport and land allocation remain the planners’ intentions; they do not establish how the completed systems will work. [Gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/10/28/new-tashkent/)

The programme also included planned presentations of furniture, craft and architecture. Afisha.uz described objects by designer Zulfizara that draw on local arcades and motifs. An installation called Mahalla Urban Living took neighbourhood spaces as a reference. The objects and the city model were scheduled in the same programme at very different scales: one can be moved, while the other proposes places where people might live. [Afisha.uz](https://www.afisha.uz/ru/gorod/2026/10/28/tashkent-design-week)

This year, ten modernist buildings and urban complexes in the existing city were added to UNESCO’s World Heritage List. University of Manchester researcher Garrett Wolf studied one of them, the Zhemchug apartment building, whose shared courtyards at three-storey intervals reinterpreted the spatial practices of a mahalla. His fieldwork also records how residents and building managers maintained and altered common spaces over time. This is research on an existing building, not a prediction that the new city will have the same outcomes. [Wallpaper*](https://www.wallpaper.com/architecture/public-buildings/unesco-world-heritage-list-modernist-additions) · [University of Manchester](https://pure.manchester.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/216118310/FULL_TEXT.PDF)

The programme has reached its scheduled end, but the model’s actual display remains unconfirmed. Understanding what future residents think about transport, services and public space still calls for interviews and observation. Following how housing, transport, public services and the environment work together would be more informative than judging the model’s appearance alone.
