# 当结构换了场景

WHEN STRUCTURE CHANGES SETTING

跑鞋的刀片变成时装轮廓，纤维从手中的小织作走进房间，品牌档案与私人收藏进入公共展厅。今天从具体材料和陈列方式出发，看看同一种结构怎样在不同场景里被重新理解。

本期包含当天发布、未来发售安排与持续展览，日期和地区分别说明。首页另列两条音乐发行线索，尚未发行的作品不作试听结论。

## 把性能结构穿成造型：Springblade 回到 Rick Owens 的鞋履里

### 中文

11月17日，Rick Owens 与 adidas 计划推出三款 Springblade 鞋履：两双 Runner 和一双高筒 Boot。它们让 adidas 2013 年推出的刀片式中底，再次进入一组以 Rick Owens 轮廓为中心的设计里。这次复出让曾被当作跑步结构的聚合物刀片，再次成为鞋履最醒目的外形特征。

Springblade 的旧方案用 16 片独立刀片替代常见泡棉中底，adidas 当时把它解释为吸收落地、再将能量导向前方的结构。设计的逻辑和视觉从一开始就绑在一起：底部不是被鞋面藏起的零件，而是整双鞋的轮廓。Rick Owens 对《Vogue》说，项目起点是他看到团队成员穿着其他品牌的跑鞋，想为自己的工作室补上这一类鞋；他也明确把 adidas 的技术视作合作理由。新系列沿用这道可见的底部结构，再把高筒靴的褶皱体量提到小腿附近。

2026 年的新款目前没有独立跑步测试或公开性能数据。2013 年与 2015 年的跑者评测曾指出旧款偏重，也质疑它在稳定性和长距离使用上的局限；这些旧测试不能替代对新鞋的检验。当前证据能确认的是三款鞋的外观、型号和发售安排，不能证明它们带来跑步性能进步。

这次复出更像一次设计语言的再编排：过去被放大的技术部件，如今成为时装轮廓的支点。它是否仍是一双好跑鞋，需要新的穿着和测试证据；现阶段更确定的，是 Springblade 以一眼可辨的底部重新获得了被观看的位置。

### English

On November 17, Rick Owens and adidas are scheduled to release three Springblade shoes: two Runners and a high Boot. The collection brings adidas’s blade-style sole, introduced in 2013, into a set of silhouettes shaped around Owens’s design language. A row of polymer blades once presented as running equipment is again the collection’s most visible design element.

The original Springblade replaced a conventional foam midsole with 16 separate blades. adidas described the structure as absorbing impact and directing energy forward. Function and appearance were tied together from the start: the sole was not hidden beneath the upper but formed the shoe’s outline. In a Vogue interview, Owens said the project began when he noticed people in his studio wearing running shoes from other brands and wanted to fill that gap. He also named adidas’s technology as a reason for working together. The new collection keeps the visible sole and extends the gathered volume of its high boot toward the calf.

There is no independent running test or public performance data for the 2026 models in the sources reviewed here. Reviews of earlier Springblade versions in 2013 and 2015 described the shoes as heavy and raised questions about stability and longer-distance use; those tests cannot establish how the new models perform. The available evidence confirms the new shoes’ forms, model identifiers and scheduled release, but it does not show a running-performance improvement.

The return is most legible as a rearrangement of design language: a once-exaggerated piece of footwear technology now anchors a fashion silhouette. Whether the new shoes run well remains an open question requiring fresh wear testing. Springblade’s distinctive underside has again been made visible.

## Sheila Hicks 在米兰把纤维从墙面推向空间

### 中文

Sheila Hicks 的《In the Thread of Love》今天在米兰 PAC 开幕，展期至 2027 年 2 月 14 日。这是她在意大利的首次回顾展，展出来自多家国际馆藏的 100 余件作品，串起六十多年的创作，也包含新作和为 PAC 户外空间制作的现场作品。ANSA 对开幕时间、规模和展期作了独立报道。

展览从早期实验一路呈现到大型装置。理解这段尺度变化，可以对照她的小型织作和公共委托：Bard Graduate Center 曾说明，她会在小作品里试验材料和手法，再把这些经验带到建筑委托和大型装置；维多利亚国家美术馆也记录了她为公共空间创作的实践。线、纤维和结被放大后，会占据房间并改变观看者与建筑的关系。

这次展览还把米兰写进作品的来路。PAC 主展将大型装置与 Ignazio Gardella 设计的建筑并置；Project Room 则回到 Hicks 1962 年在米兰初遇画家 Piero Dorazio 的记忆，展出两人档案材料、Dorazio 赠予她的三件作品，以及献给他的现场创作。这里的城市联系由具体的相遇、信件和作品组成，不只是展签上的地名。

Hicks 曾谈到重力如何参与作品：纤维可以垂落、堆叠，也会在碰到地面时改变形态。观展时可以留意色块怎样由线的密度长出来，以及装置怎样顺着墙角、地面和户外光线展开。她的作品跨越绘画、纺织、雕塑与建筑；米兰这次回顾展提供了一个具体入口，去看材料如何带着尺度和场所一起变化。

### English

Sheila Hicks’s *In the Thread of Love* opens today at Milan’s PAC and runs through February 14, 2027. It is her first Italian retrospective, bringing together more than 100 works from international collections across six decades, including new work and a site-specific piece for PAC’s outdoor space. ANSA independently reports the opening, scale and closing date.

The exhibition traces a path from early experiments to large installations. To understand that change in scale, it helps to look at Hicks’s smaller weavings alongside her public commissions: Bard Graduate Center describes how she tested materials and techniques in small works before carrying them into architectural commissions and large-scale pieces; the National Gallery of Victoria also documents her work in public spaces. Enlarged, thread, fiber and knots occupy rooms and change how viewers meet the architecture.

Milan also enters the story of the work. The main exhibition sets large installations against the building designed by Ignazio Gardella. In the Project Room, Hicks returns to her first meeting with painter Piero Dorazio in Milan in 1962. The display brings together archival material, three works Dorazio gave her and a site-specific work dedicated to him. The city connection is grounded in an encounter, letters and artworks, rather than a place name on a label.

Hicks has spoken about gravity as a participant in her work: fibers can hang, pile up and change shape when they meet the floor. Visitors can watch how color emerges from the density of the threads, and how installations respond to corners, floors and outdoor light. Her practice moves across painting, textiles, sculpture and architecture; the Milan retrospective offers a concrete way to see how material changes with scale and place.

## 把「黑人美学」拆成复数：伦敦设计博物馆如何呈现差异

### 中文

伦敦设计博物馆的《The Nue Black Aesthetic》11月6日开幕，展出16位设计师的100多件作品，涵盖时装、家具、建筑、产品设计与雕塑。策展人 Charlene Prempeh 用“Nue”借指 nuance（细微差异），把视线放在不同设计师如何将各自的历史、材料和生活经验带进作品。

展览的六个部分围绕身份如何成为灵感、设计如何构成社群，以及设计师怎样回应既有体系展开。Taiba Akhuetie 把常被放进身体和身份讨论的合成发丝编进家具；Bianca Saunders 的西装曾由 Usher 在 2023 年 Met Gala 穿着；Giles Tettey Nartey 重新演绎西非木琴 balafon；Mac Collins 的《Open Code》把多米诺骨牌桌与牙买加社群生活、Harewood House 的奴隶制历史并置。材料、形式与历史入口各不相同。

展名也接回两段不同的历史讨论。Larry Neal 在 1968 年的《The Black Arts Movement》中，把艺术与黑人社群、自我定义紧密相连。Trey Ellis 在 1989 年的《The New Black Aesthetic》中，描述当时的黑人创作者如何从多种文化经验中借用、重组并跨越既有类型。两篇文章各自回应不同年代，不能代替今天设计师的表态；它们说明“谁来定义黑人美学”一直是被讨论的问题。

Prempeh 说，她不希望非洲蜡染成为人们辨认非洲设计的唯一图像；Ini Archibong 则追问，把作品统称为“Black aesthetic”，会不会把设计师放进一个由外部规定的类别。Taiba Akhuetie 也谈到，她不想每次被介绍时都先被归为“有才华的黑人艺术家”。这些设计师的说法各有侧重。

展览最有价值的部分，是把分类问题放回具体作品旁边。有的作品带着家族、地域或社群线索，有的设计师更在意形式，也有人担心身份标签先于作品抵达观众。这组作品和立场值得继续讨论。现有证据还无法证明，一场博物馆展览已经改变设计行业如何收藏、书写或评价这些作品。

### English

The Design Museum in London opened The Nue Black Aesthetic on November 6. The exhibition brings together more than 100 objects by 16 designers across fashion, furniture, architecture, product design and sculpture. Curator Charlene Prempeh uses “Nue” to signal nuance and attends to the different histories, materials and lived experiences that shape each practice.

Its six sections look at identity as inspiration, design as a way of building community, and designers’ responses to established systems. Taiba Akhuetie weaves synthetic hair, often discussed through the body and identity, into furniture. Bianca Saunders designed a suit worn by Usher at the 2023 Met Gala. Giles Tettey Nartey reinterprets the West African balafon. Mac Collins’s Open Code places a domino table alongside histories of Jamaican communal life and Harewood House’s links to slavery. The works take different routes through material, form and history.

The exhibition also points back to two historical discussions. In his 1968 essay The Black Arts Movement, Larry Neal links art to Black community and self-definition. In his 1989 essay The New Black Aesthetic, Trey Ellis describes Black creators drawing on multiple cultural experiences and crossing established genres. Each text responds to its own period and cannot stand in for designers speaking today. Together, they show that who defines Black aesthetics has a longer history.

Prempeh says she does not want wax print to remain the only image through which people recognize African design. Ini Archibong asks whether grouping work under “Black aesthetic” can place designers into a category defined from outside. Taiba Akhuetie also describes her frustration at being introduced first as a “talented Black artist.” These designers speak from different positions.

The exhibition’s strongest contribution is to keep the question beside specific works. Some pieces carry references to family, place or community; some designers emphasize form; others worry that an identity label may reach the audience before the work does. These works and positions invite further discussion. Current evidence cannot show that one museum exhibition has changed how the design industry collects, writes about or evaluates them.

## 把私人收藏变成一条通往韩国艺术的路径

### 中文

SFMOMA 的《RM x SFMOMA: Between You and Me》从 10 月 3 日展至 2027 年 2 月 7 日，汇集近 180 件来自 RM 与博物馆的作品。这是 RM 私人收藏首次以博物馆展览形式呈现，也只有这一站美国展期。RM 与 SFMOMA 策展人 América Castillo、Hyoeun Kim 共同策划；展览由一份私人收藏出发，却让韩国艺术家与不同年代、地域的作品并置。

RM 将收藏形容为一段很长的自我介绍。展厅文字保留他的手写字迹，特别语音导览由他本人讲述；他也参与撰写韩英双语展签和选择空间音乐。八个单元从蓝色、自然与居所，走到人物和创作热情，把观看路径放在情感、生活经验和作品之间的联系上。观众不需要先掌握完整艺术史，便能从一件触动自己的作品进入展览。

策展的连接关系落在具体作品上。比如，SFMOMA 收藏的白南准佛像电视墨稿，与 RM 收藏的《Enlightenment Compressed》并置；两件作品隔着时间谈科技、信仰和观看。其他单元把韩国艺术家的绘画、陶艺与雕塑放在国际艺术家的作品之间，让熟悉与陌生互相照亮。策展人向《Christie’s》表示，他们希望观众看到韩国艺术跨越多个时期与风格的层次，而不是只把它归入单一类别。

这条入口仍带有明确的个人视角。近 180 件作品构成的是 RM 与博物馆共同选择的一种叙述，不是韩国艺术的完整样本；SFMOMA 把私人收藏放入公共展厅，让个人偏好成为遇见现代与当代韩国艺术的一种路径。展览位于七层，需另购附加票：工作日 10 美元，周末及假日 12 美元。

### English

SFMOMA’s “RM x SFMOMA: Between You and Me” runs from October 3 through February 7, 2027, bringing together nearly 180 works from RM’s and the museum’s collections. It is the first museum exhibition of RM’s personal collection and its only U.S. presentation. RM co-curated the show with SFMOMA curators América Castillo and Hyoeun Kim. A private collection is the starting point, while Korean artists are placed alongside works from different periods and places.

RM describes a collection as a long self-introduction. His handwritten texts appear in the galleries, and he narrates a special audio guide. The Korea Times reports that he also wrote Korean and English exhibition texts and selected the music. The eight sections move from blue, nature and home to people and artistic passion, connecting the works through feelings and everyday experience. Visitors can begin with a work that holds their attention without first knowing a complete history of art.

The curatorial connections become visible in specific pairings. A Nam June Paik ink drawing of a Buddha watching television from SFMOMA’s collection appears beside “Enlightenment Compressed,” a 1994 sculpture from RM’s collection; together, the works bring technology, belief and looking into the same room. Other sections place Korean painting, ceramics and sculpture among works by artists from elsewhere. The curators told Christie’s that they wanted visitors to encounter Korean art as a layered tradition across periods and styles, rather than as a single category.

This route carries a clear personal point of view. The nearly 180 works form one account chosen by RM and the museum, not a complete survey of Korean art. By placing a private collection in its public galleries, SFMOMA makes personal taste one way into modern and contemporary Korean art. The exhibition is on Floor 7 and requires a separate surcharge: $10 on weekdays and $12 on weekends and holidays.
