# 留下来的，是下一次使用

WHAT STAYS IN USE

把旧衣重新染色，把舞台录音重新编排，把道路变成临时展场，把衣服稳稳地支撑在展柜里：这些工作改变了作品怎样继续出现。今天从苏黎世的灯光展览出发，经过纽约、多哈、东京、伦敦、巴黎和香港，再回到两份重新进入发行日程的音乐。

有些项目刚刚开放，有些快闪已经结束。各篇保留具体日期，提供仍可参与的安排，也回看材料和制作留下的线索。

## 125盏家用灯，怎样变成一条能走进去的光隧道

### 中文

125盏IKEA环形灯被接成11米长的通道，八盏接近三米高的Mathmos熔岩灯被镜墙反复映出。Sabine Marcelis在苏黎世的首个博物馆展《Light and Color》，把家里能单独放置的灯具，重新组织成需要人走进去的空间。展览10月30日开幕，在Museum für Gestaltung Zürich的Toni-Areal展至2027年4月4日。[馆方详情](https://museum-gestaltung.ch/de/ausstellung/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color) · [10月31日的策展采访](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/design-events/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color-zurich-museum-fur-gestaltung-exhibition-review)

这次组织方式值得看。策展人Damian Fopp向Wallpaper解释，他们不想把物件摆上台座就结束，而要让两种灯具形成完整的空间。灯的轮廓仍然认得出来，重复、镜面和观看距离改变了它的尺度。镜头能截走一片橙光，现场的身体却必须经历整条通道。

Marcelis一直从材料试验开始工作。她在German Design Council的访谈中说，自己通常不先画出最终形状，而要在工坊里观察材料怎样成形；光、场地和观者的位置都会改变结果。2022年接受Salone del Mobile采访时，她也把形状的产生与最初的材料加工联系起来。苏黎世的空间实验有这段持续的制作方法作支撑。[展前访谈](https://www.german-design-council.de/en/design-perspectives/article-detail/ich-gestalte-die-beziehung-zwischen-material-und-licht) · [材料与形状](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/sabine-marcelis-and-less-more-interview)

这种尺度转换也能从旧项目里看清。IKEA在2023年的原始发布中，把橙玻璃环灯写作可摆在桌上、也可挂墙的家用物件；今年NGV策展人Gemma Savio的长文则记录，Marcelis与CLOUD怎样用镜面走廊和逐室变化的颜色组织Cartier展览。前者处理一个物件怎样放，后者处理人怎样走。[环灯原始用途](https://www.ikea.com/global/en/newsroom/collaborations/ikea-and-sabine-marcelis-launch-the-varmblixt-collection-exploring-sculptural-design-and-light-230117/) · [馆方策展说明](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/essay/matter-and-light/)

Mathmos装置也保留着产品开发的关系。Marcelis说，第一次和厂方见面时，她就想做一盏巨大的熔岩灯。展厅中的Column灯高296厘米，粉色蜡体缓慢升降；同一色调还被用于计划11月推出的小型Astro灯。装置和家用物件因此会互相返回，生产合作没有藏在视觉效果后面。[灯具合作采访](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/lighting/sabine-marcelis-bubblegum-pink-lava-lamp-for-mathmos)

《Sunbeam Captured》则把运动放到了物件外面。Marcelis与Boris Acket合作，用机械制造水波，让移动光源经过水面，再由渐变镜面接住反光。它和她4月为Coachella设计的充气迷宫，有不同的材料和用途，后者以墙体遮阳，给音乐节观众留下停留的空间。两者都需要把光线和人的位置放进设计条件里。[水与光的装置](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/design-events/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color-zurich-museum-fur-gestaltung-exhibition-review) · [Coachella项目采访](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/sabine-marcelis-maze-coachella-2026)

这些看似干净的形状，背后有工厂调色、工程和原型。ICON曾在她的工作室记录树脂与颜料的混合；Family Style的采访里，她把让成品「看起来容易」之前的开发按月计算。看这场展览时，材料样品和制作线索值得和镜面房间放在一起看。灯具把空间点亮，做出那束光的人和步骤也该留在视野里。[工厂调色](https://www.iconeye.com/design/sabine-marcelis-materials-colour-collaboration-ikea) · [工程与原型](https://www.family.style/articles/sabine-marcelis-designer-interview)

### English

An 11-metre passage made from 125 IKEA ring lamps and a mirrored room holding eight nearly three-metre Mathmos lava lamps turn familiar objects into spaces to enter. Sabine Marcelis’s first museum exhibition, *Light and Color*, opened on 30 October at the Museum für Gestaltung Zürich’s Toni-Areal location and continues through 4 April 2027.[Museum information](https://museum-gestaltung.ch/de/ausstellung/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color) · [Interview published on 31 October](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/design-events/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color-zurich-museum-fur-gestaltung-exhibition-review)

The arrangement is worth attending to. Curator Damian Fopp told Wallpaper that they wanted to do more than put objects on pedestals: two lamps would become complete spaces. Their outlines remain recognizable, while repetition, mirrors and viewing distance alter their scale. A photograph can isolate a patch of orange light; a visitor’s body has to move along the passage.

Marcelis has long started with material experiments. In her German Design Council interview, she described working in the workshop rather than drawing a finished shape first. Light, surroundings and the viewer’s position affect the result. In Salone del Mobile’s 2022 interview, she likewise connected emerging forms with the first stages of working a material. The Zurich installations extend this sustained method.[Pre-exhibition interview](https://www.german-design-council.de/en/design-perspectives/article-detail/ich-gestalte-die-beziehung-zwischen-material-und-licht) · [Material and form](https://www.salonemilano.it/en/articles/sabine-marcelis-and-less-more-interview)

Earlier projects make that change of scale concrete. IKEA’s original 2023 release describes the orange-glass ring lamp as a domestic object for a table or wall. This year, NGV curator Gemma Savio records how Marcelis and CLOUD organized the Cartier exhibition through a mirrored walkway and rooms with changing colour. One account concerns where an object can be placed; the other, how people move.[The lamp’s original use](https://www.ikea.com/global/en/newsroom/collaborations/ikea-and-sabine-marcelis-launch-the-varmblixt-collection-exploring-sculptural-design-and-light-230117/) · [Museum curatorial account](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/essay/matter-and-light/)

The Mathmos installation retains its connection to product development. Marcelis said a giant lava lamp was her ambition at their first meeting. The exhibition’s Column lamps stand 296 centimetres tall, with pink wax slowly rising and falling. A smaller Astro lamp in the same colour is planned for November. The installation and the domestic object remain part of the same manufacturing relationship.[Interview about the collaboration](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/lighting/sabine-marcelis-bubblegum-pink-lava-lamp-for-mathmos)

In *Sunbeam Captured*, movement occurs outside the object. Marcelis and Boris Acket use mechanically generated waves, a moving light and a gradient mirror to produce changing reflections. Her inflatable maze at Coachella in April used a different material and served another purpose: its walls supplied shade and places to pause. Both projects make light and a person’s position conditions of the design.[Water and light](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/design-events/sabine-marcelis-light-and-color-zurich-museum-fur-gestaltung-exhibition-review) · [Coachella interview](https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/sabine-marcelis-maze-coachella-2026)

Simple-looking forms depend on colour mixing, engineering and prototypes. ICON reported from the workshop where resin and pigments were being mixed; in Family Style, Marcelis described months of development before an object could look effortless. The samples and making processes deserve attention alongside the mirrored rooms. They keep the work and the people producing the light within the exhibition’s field of view.[Colour mixing](https://www.iconeye.com/design/sabine-marcelis-materials-colour-collaboration-ikea) · [Engineering and prototypes](https://www.family.style/articles/sabine-marcelis-designer-interview)

## 纽约快闪结束后，Our Legacy 与 Nike 的两种产品路径

### 中文

纽约 Procell 的 Sport Shop NYC 已于 11 月 1 日结束。Our Legacy Work Shop 与 Nike 把三类东西放进这间店：Procell 的档案衣橱、既有 Work Shop Sport 系列，以及由 Nike re:create 寻来的美产库存服装，后者在斯德哥尔摩经过重新染色与改造。这场 10 月 30 日开始的快闪，是双方正式跑步系列到来前的一次独立活动。

这个区别会影响我们怎样看现场的衣服。Highsnobiety 的 Jake Silbert 在店内采访 Jockum Hallin，明确把活动与未来的 Nike 联名系列分开；报道中的连帽衫经过改染、加入华夫格针织内衬，再印上此次影像的图案。不能把这些旧物重制，当成 2027 年技术服装与鞋履已经完整亮相。

Procell 的参与也有具体分工。Brian Procell 和 Jessica Gonsalves 在 GORE-TEX 的历史访谈里说，店内商品逐件挑选和整理，他们同时为设计团队提供服装参考；GQ 的 2018 年采访也把幕后咨询列为这门生意的重要部分。放在这里，档案衣物既是可穿的旧物，也是研究比例、材质和穿着方式的材料。两次历史访谈不能证明这场快闪卖得好，却能解释这个场地与项目为何有关。

Work Shop Sport 则早已有自己的产品路径。2025 年首发报道记录了日常与运动之间的穿着定位；2026 年春季，Hallin 在另一场采访中谈到余料、库存技术面料以及反复调整版型的方法。SportsVerse 的原始采访也记录，他当时就把自有运动系列看作未来合作的基础。此前与 Armani、C.P. Company 的项目分别涉及档案面料、残余布料和按单品选择的制作工艺，这些经验不能一概称为回收同一种旧衣。

真正的新产品还在后面。BoF 10 月 26 日的独家采访确认，Nike 与 Our Legacy Work Shop 计划在 2027 年春推出男女技术服装和全新鞋履；Nike 的 Jarrett Reynolds 说，双方从头开发产品。Hallin 希望把技术藏进更适合日常的外观里，这是设计目标，目前还没有完整产品与实测结果可以验证。

这场快闪留下的线索，比一件联名印花具体：同一套合作可以先处理已经存在的衣服，再开发尚不存在的产品。前者的材料和改造过程已有记录；后者仍要等到鞋型、结构与穿着表现真正公开，才能继续判断。

### English

Sport Shop NYC at Procell ended on November 1. Our Legacy Work Shop and Nike brought together Procell’s archival wardrobe, the existing Work Shop Sport line and US-made Nike deadstock sourced by Nike re:create and reworked in Stockholm. The October 30–November 1 pop-up was a standalone activation ahead of the partners’ future running collection.

That distinction changes how the clothes should be read. Interviewing Jockum Hallin in the store, Highsnobiety’s Jake Silbert explicitly separated the Nike-powered activation from the future collaboration. His report describes overdyed hoodies with waffle-knit linings and campaign graphics. These reworked garments are not a complete reveal of the technical apparel and footwear planned for 2027.

Procell has a specific role in this arrangement. In a historical GORE-TEX interview, Brian Procell and Jessica Gonsalves describe hand-picking and restoring garments while supplying references to design teams. GQ’s 2018 interview similarly identifies behind-the-scenes consultancy as central to the business. The archive provides material for studying proportion and construction as well as clothes to wear. Those interviews explain the venue’s relevance; they establish nothing about this pop-up’s sales.

Work Shop Sport already had its own product trajectory. The 2025 launch feature describes active-friendly everyday clothing. In a separate spring 2026 interview, Hallin discusses residual and deadstock technical fabrics and revisiting familiar cuts. His earlier SportsVerse interview positioned the in-house line as a foundation for future partnerships. Previous Armani and C.P. Company collaborations involved different combinations of archive fabrics, leftover cloth and item-specific production methods, rather than a single generic recycling process.

The new products remain ahead. BoF’s October 26 exclusive confirms plans for men’s and women’s technical apparel and new footwear in spring 2027. Nike’s Jarrett Reynolds describes development from scratch. Hallin’s intention is to place performance technology within a quieter everyday appearance. The complete products and independent wear tests are not yet available to assess that goal.

The pop-up makes a useful distinction visible: a partnership can first work with clothing that already exists, then develop products that do not. Materials and alterations can be examined in the first stage. The second still requires actual shoe designs, construction details and evidence from wearing them.

## 一场演出结束后，Makaya McCraven怎样再做一遍

### 中文

10月30日，Makaya McCraven推出带加收曲《Bright Lights》的《Off the Record》数字版。这是2025年实体发行的一周年延续，素材仍来自此前的现场录音，不能把它写成今年新录的一张专辑。International Anthem的说明把新曲、数字入口和11月13日开始的美国演出分别列出。[厂牌本次说明](https://www.intlanthem.com/news/makaya-mccraven-marks-off-the-record-one-year-anniversary-w-digital-release-feat-new-bonus-track-us-shows-start-november-13)

这套作品由四张EP构成，连接不同城市、年份和乐手组合。值得注意的是，现场录音只是原料。McCraven在OndaRock的采访里谈到，录音室里的剪辑本身就是创作；在KUVO公开访谈逐字稿里，他又谈到，集体即兴经过后制可以成为新的作曲工具，再被带回现场继续发展。正式制作记录还把不同会话和后制时间逐项列开；《Bright Lights》的录音来自2017年6月13日的Hideout。一次演出有了被再次安排的机会。

这种方法并不适用于他所有作品。Emma Warren为Composer Magazine所作的原访，区分了《In These Times》长年发展、已经写出的曲目，与此前从即兴录音中取样的工作线。读《Off the Record》时，把制作方式说清楚，比用一个“即兴爵士”的标签概括全部更有帮助。Pitchfork的评论也分别讨论四张EP，而非把不同时期的录音当成同一场演出的连续记录。

对重新编排的效果，评论者并没有给出完全一致的答案。HHV的Stephanie Grimm关注即兴、采样和共同记忆之间的关系；HHV同时经营唱片零售，这份评论的商业位置需要保留。Written in Music的Dick Hovenga与In Search of Media的逐曲评论，则比较了不同组合留下的节奏与织体。这里引用的是他们的听感，不是我们补写的亲听经验。

Bedroomdisco的Delia Pauli提出另一面：四组材料连成专辑，也可能带来段落之间的跳跃。这与把现场变成一张完整唱片的目标之间，留下了可继续讨论的距离。艺术家的工作理念和评论者的判断，各自说明的范围并不相同。

这次数字发行的价值，首先在于多了一个可以进入这些作品的渠道。新的《Bright Lights》和旧录音的不同组织方式，给了继续比较的理由：房间、乐手和剪辑各留下了什么。它没有抹掉2025年的实体版本，也不需要靠“全新专辑”的说法来成立。

### English

On October 30, Makaya McCraven added a digital edition of Off the Record, including the bonus track Bright Lights. It marks a year since the 2025 physical release and draws on earlier live recordings; it is not a newly recorded 2026 album. International Anthem’s announcement distinguishes the digital release, the additional track and US dates beginning November 13.[Current label announcement](https://www.intlanthem.com/news/makaya-mccraven-marks-off-the-record-one-year-anniversary-w-digital-release-feat-new-bonus-track-us-shows-start-november-13)

Four EPs connect different cities, years and groups of musicians. Live recordings supply material for another stage of composition. In OndaRock’s interview, McCraven describes studio editing as creative work in its own right. In KUVO’s public interview transcript, he describes collective improvisation becoming material for studio composition, then returning to performance. The formal credits separately identify sessions and later editing: Bright Lights was recorded at the Hideout on June 13, 2017. An event can be organized again after it has ended.

That process does not describe his entire output. Emma Warren’s interview for Composer Magazine distinguishes In These Times’ long-developed written compositions from his earlier work sampling improvisations. For Off the Record, identifying the process is more useful than letting an improvisational-jazz label cover every decision. Pitchfork’s review also considers the four EPs separately rather than treating recordings from different periods as one uninterrupted concert.

Critical responses to the rearrangement differ. HHV’s Stephanie Grimm examines improvisation, sampling and collective memory; HHV also sells records, a commercial relationship that remains relevant. Dick Hovenga in Written in Music and the track-by-track review in In Search of Media compare the rhythms and textures of the different ensembles. Those are their listening judgments, not experiences we claim for ourselves.

Bedroomdisco’s Delia Pauli offers a reservation: assembling four contexts can leave the album feeling episodic. That leaves a useful tension with the ambition to turn events into a complete album. An artist’s account of a method and a critic’s assessment make different kinds of claims.

The digital edition first supplies another route into this body of work. Bright Lights and the contrasting arrangements of older recordings give listeners something specific to compare: what the room, the players and the edit each retain. The release leaves the 2025 physical edition intact. Its value does not depend on presenting the recordings as an entirely new album.

## KK 线主会场明天结束：一条车道怎样成为五天的展览

### 中文

DESIGNART TOKYO 的 KK 线主会场将于明天 11 月 3 日结束，比整个艺术节的 11 月 8 日提前五天。今天刊出的 TECTURE MAG 现场报道，把这场五日限定的展览写得更具体：旧收费站用作入口接待，路面上出现展览帐篷、设计商店、舞台和咖啡区。这次展览处在道路用途转换的过程中。东京都把停路至部分区间开放之间列为过渡使用阶段，今年4月也已安排步行、跑步与电影等体验；永久公共空间的改造仍在后面。[城市改造与试行记录](https://www.toshiseibi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/machizukuri/machi_project/toshi_saisei/kk_arikata)

ALTEMY 负责人津川惠理向采访媒体解释，设计必须回应道路法规、不同管理辖区，以及电力和厕所等接待条件。路锥、箭头板和简易构件被用来引导人的动线。保留的道路设施因此有了新的使用方法，同时也让“把车道交还给人”从一句口号，落到入口、停留位置和基础设施这些具体问题上。

展品也可以沿着“怎样改变观看”来挑选。Amana、FIGLAB 与 Qosmo 的《MOIRÉ》把不同图像生成方式放在一起：有借助 Stable Diffusion 讨论摄影的作品，也有用镜子和水让倒影发生变化的《Kyo》。Konel 与 X Vision 的《Landscape Superimpose LED》则把透光屏幕中的影像与实景叠合。ICOMA 展出的机器人盆栽臂和可折叠电动摩托，把身体与移动的问题带回这段原本供车辆通行的道路。

旁边的 AUTOBAHN – The Design Store 提供另一种接近作品的方式。YAMAGIWA 展示需要手动展开灯罩的 peco-poco 等照明。商店空间由 GROUP 设计，选品由 Jean-Luc Colonna d’Istria 策划，与 ALTEMY 负责的整体场地设计需要分开看。日常灯具、可以动手使用的产品和装置在这里并置，不必都以展墙后的艺术品来理解。

离开 KK 线，节目的尺度又会变化。代官山的 Nomura 展览讨论空间拆除后的材料分离与再利用；六本木 Ambientec Gallery 展出可维修结构和重新整备的灯具。Tokyo Midtown 的《BONE VOYAGE》通过骨传导装置改变听觉，《Tempura Object》把非食品材料裹粉油炸，作为造型过程。这些场地各有自己的日期与参与规则，不能把主会场的门票和截止时间套在所有展示上。

如果明天前往 KK 线，主会场最终日到 17:00，最后入场为 16:00。13:00–14:00 的末场对谈围绕技术与创作展开，按已公布安排需要预约，并另持主会场门票；是否还有名额应查预约页。比起一次看完所有展项，这条路线更适合带着一个具体问题走：一件熟悉的材料、工具或公共设施，改变使用方法后，究竟多提供了什么？

### English

DESIGNART TOKYO’s KK Line main venue closes tomorrow, November 3, five days before the wider festival ends on November 8. Today’s TECTURE MAG report describes a five-day temporary arrangement: a former tollbooth becomes reception, with exhibition tents, a design store, a stage and a café on the disused road. The metropolitan government’s policy treats the interval before sections reopen as a transitional phase. Its April programme had already tested walking, running and film events here. Permanent conversion into public space remains ahead.[Policy and trial-use record](https://www.toshiseibi.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/machizukuri/machi_project/toshi_saisei/kk_arikata)

ALTEMY’s Eri Tsugawa describes constraints involving road regulations, different authorities, electricity and toilets. Cones, arrow signs and simple components establish routes for pedestrians. Retained road infrastructure gains another function, making the shift from vehicles to people a question of entrances, places to pause and basic visitor facilities.

The works offer different ways to examine perception. Amana, FIGLAB and Qosmo’s MOIRÉ brings together distinct image-making systems, including a Stable Diffusion work addressing photography and Kyo’s shifting mirror-and-water reflections. Konel and X Vision’s Landscape Superimpose LED overlays imagery with a real view through a transparent display. ICOMA’s robotic bonsai arm and folding electric bike return questions of bodies and movement to a road once built for vehicles.

AUTOBAHN – The Design Store provides another way into the objects. YAMAGIWA’s selection includes peco-poco, whose shade unfolds by hand. GROUP designed the store and Jean-Luc Colonna d’Istria curated it; those roles are distinct from ALTEMY’s overall venue design.
