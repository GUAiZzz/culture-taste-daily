# 材料怎么留下来

What Materials Keep

布鲁塞尔把雪铁龙车库改成美术馆，深圳把古漆器和当代漆艺放在同一层展厅。金泽的工艺展沿酒店客房展开，银座把 180 色针织样布拿出来看。两条音乐线索也能听到做法的差异，从 1969 年的现场录音，到 Peter Gabriel 给一首歌留下的两个混音。

## 布鲁塞尔把雪铁龙车库打开给观众

### 中文

11 月 28 日，布鲁塞尔 KANAL 将在运河旁的旧雪铁龙车库开门。玻璃围合的大陈列厅留下来了，车库横向延展的车间也仍能辨认。博物馆把新展厅放进这片工业建筑，再用餐饮、演出和公共空间把它们串起来。11 月 25 日，首批进馆的观众是与馆方合作的学校里的学生。

开馆周末从周六上午十点延续到周日晚十点。走进建筑免费，十个开幕展览另有分时预约。这个区别会改变参观动线。有人可以专程看一场展，也有人只进大厅、走上一层，在熟悉的街区先认一认这个新地方。

在采访和开幕资料里，设计团队反复谈到车库原先就有的尺度。留住那片大空间，也就留住了人在楼里自行停留和决定往哪走的余地。开馆之后值得留意的，是当地人会把它用成哪一种日常场所。

### English

KANAL opens on November 28 in a former Citroën garage beside the Brussels canal. The glass showroom is still there, as are the broad horizontal volumes of the workshops. New galleries sit within the industrial complex alongside spaces for food, performance and daily public use. On November 25 pupils from schools working with KANAL were among the first visitors.

Opening weekend runs from Saturday at ten in the morning to Sunday at ten at night. The building is free to enter, with timed reservations for the ten opening exhibitions. The distinction affects how people can use the place. One visitor may come specifically for an exhibition. Another can walk into the hall, go upstairs and get to know a new interior in a familiar neighbourhood.

In interviews and opening material, the design team returns to the scale the garage already had. Retaining that space leaves visitors more room to pause and decide where to go. After the opening, it will be worth seeing what kind of everyday place people in Brussels make of it.

## 典亚艺博深圳首展，可以沿着漆器看

### 中文

典亚艺博 11 月 26 日在深圳市当代艺术与城市规划馆举行首展，27 日至 29 日向公众开放。逾 30 家海内外画廊和古董商把家具、漆器、水墨和珠宝带进同一层展厅。此前长期在香港举办的展会，这次把一部分熟悉的收藏门类带进了深圳。

可以从漆器看起。罗汉堂把不同时期的古漆器和当代作品放进同一组展陈。土壤文创展出甘而可的犀皮漆，也带来隗楠的《结》系列。隗楠先把皮革折出褶皱，再用层层深红色大漆定形。原本来自不同展位的物件因此有了一条看得见的关系。多次髹漆累积起来的层次，如何通过打磨露出来，又怎样落在完全不同的器形上？

展会另设「革古鼎新」，把旧器物和当代艺术、流行文化放在一起。光说「古今对话」还很难看见作品。沿着材料和技法在展厅里走一遍，会更容易发现哪一种做法还在延续，哪一种用途已经改变。

### English

Fine Art Asia held the opening of its first Shenzhen edition on November 26 at the Shenzhen Museum of Contemporary Art and Urban Planning. Public dates run from November 27 to 29. More than thirty galleries and dealers bring furniture, lacquer, ink painting and jewellery to the same floor. The fair has long been held in Hong Kong and brings several of its familiar collecting fields to Shenzhen.

Lacquer offers a point of entry. Luohan Tang presents lacquer from different periods alongside contemporary work. The Gallery by SOIL shows Gan Erke’s xipi lacquer alongside Wei Nan’s Knot series. Wei folds the leather first, then fixes its shape with coats of deep-red lacquer. The display makes a relation visible between things shown at different stands. Look at how coats of lacquer are built up, what sanding reveals and how the resulting surface changes across different forms.

Another presentation places old objects beside contemporary art and popular culture. Following a material or technique across the fair gives that encounter more definition. It makes it possible to see which methods continue and which uses have shifted.

### English

Each gallery can make its own selection legible, while visitors can compare it with what they have just seen down the hall.

One way to visit is to see two rooms using similar materials in succession. The size, weight and placement of a cup, a dish or a sculpture are easier to consider at the scale of a room. The encounter between the craft and the room is part of the fair.

I would make time for the workshop early in the visit. A stone has to be polished and held in place before the finished piece can sit on a hand. With a sense of that work, it becomes easier to compare the cuts, settings and small differences in weight when looking at finished pieces.

## 无印良品在银座展出 180 色针织样布

### 中文

11 月 28 日，ATELIER MUJI GINZA 开出「无印良品的基本服」系列第三展，这次看颜色。两间展厅里，一边是材料本身的颜色，一边从蓝、苏芳与黄檗出发，把不同颜色的羊毛混合，做成一组 180 色的针织色样。

良品计划在展览资料里给出一条能循着走的过程，从植物、原毛和纱线，一直看到织片与成衣。苏芳和黄檗在日本历史染织中各有自己的用途，正仓院展的术语资料也能查到它们作为染料的记录。展厅把这些知识落在针脚上，观众可以看色样是怎样一点点偏向黄、红或蓝的。

看服装时，色名往往是最后贴上去的标签。这次陈列把标签之前的工作放出来。站在材料和成衣中间，会比较容易理解「自然色」这个说法究竟指材料原有的颜色，还是一项由人反复调整过的设计。

### English

ATELIER MUJI GINZA opens the third exhibition in its Clothing Basics series on November 28. The subject is colour. One part follows colour already present in materials. Another starts with indigo, sappanwood and amur cork tree, mixing coloured wool into a set of 180 knitted samples.

Ryohin Keikaku lays out a sequence from plants through fibre, yarn, fabric and finally garments. Sappanwood and amur cork tree have documented uses in historical Japanese dyeing. The Shōsō-in exhibition’s glossary gives an account of both. At Ginza those materials can be considered alongside actual stitches, where the eye can follow small changes toward yellow, red or blue.

On clothes, a colour name is a label applied quite late. This exhibition makes the work before the label visible. The material and the finished garment give viewers a way to ask whether a colour comes from the fibre itself or from repeated decisions about how to use it.

## Peter Gabriel 为《o\i》留下两套混音

### 中文

同时发行不同混音，是他在 2023 年《i/o》已经用过的方法。同一首歌在不同版本里，谁先被听见、某件乐器离人有多远，都会改变句子的重心。新专辑依然有单独的 Bright-Side 与 Dark-Side 碟，也有相应的数字版本，听众可以直接来回切换。

发行周期结束后，这道听觉题才完整出现。我会从《Yes, It Will Go》的人声位置听起。歌词和演唱都熟悉以后，再去留意混音让哪一层声音更接近你。两版之间的差别，通常先出现在这些细处。

### English

Peter Gabriel releases the full album *o\i* today. On the November 24 full moon he shared the Bright-Side Mix of its penultimate track, “Yes, It Will Go”, mixed by Mark “Spike” Stent. Tchad Blake’s Dark-Side Mix is also on the album. Its separate track release is timed for the next new moon.

Gabriel used the same approach on *i/o* in 2023. Different mixes of a song can shift which sound reaches you first and how close an instrument feels, changing the emphasis of a phrase. The new release gives the Bright-Side and Dark-Side mixes their own discs and digital versions, making it possible to switch between them.

The end of the release cycle opens up a listening question. Start with where the voice sits in “Yes, It Will Go”. Once the lyric and performance are familiar, notice which layer the mix moves closer. That is where the difference between the two versions can first be heard.

The cultural heritage catalogue attributes the latter to Ōkyo and records the special viewer that accompanied it.

These works arrange the viewer at two scales. The lens fixes the position of the eye and gives a sheet of paper depth. The Peacock Room puts the viewer inside the image’s dimensions, where turning brings another run of branches into view. In the exhibition, follow one branch and see where a frame interrupts it and where the painting picks it up again.

## Patti Smith 把野外录音带到米兰的展厅和剧院

### 中文

11 月 26 日，Patti Smith 和 Soundwalk Collective 的《Correspondences》在米兰三年展馆开幕。乔治·加贝尔抒情剧院排了 26、27 日两场演出，美术馆里的展览持续到明年 3 月 7 日。

作品从野外录音开始。Soundwalk Collective 在不同地点采集声音，在工作室把 Patti Smith 的声音叠进录音和新写的声响里。米兰这站把影像两两并置，也摆出从录音地点带回的物件和档案材料。展览没有按年份排作品。听到某段录音以后，观众可以看向相邻的影像，也可以从一件实物回去找声音原来的地方。

今年夏天，法国阿尔勒那一站加入与卡马格湿地有关的新委托。到了米兰，艺术家又从达·芬奇《大西洋古抄本》取材。这两次变化都发生在同一个持续多年的项目里。地方留下来的东西具体到一份录音、一张纸或一个影像片段，换一座城，还能多出一种把它们放在一起的方法。

### English

Correspondences, by Patti Smith and Soundwalk Collective, opened at Triennale Milano on November 26. Teatro Lirico Giorgio Gaber scheduled two performances for November 26 and 27, while the exhibition runs through March 7, 2027.

The work begins with field recordings. Soundwalk Collective gathers sound on location and layers Smith’s voice with those recordings and new compositions in the studio. The Milan exhibition pairs films and shows physical traces and archival material brought back from recording sites. The works are arranged without a chronology. After hearing one recording, a visitor can turn to a neighbouring film or follow an object back towards the place a sound came from.

In Arles this summer the artists added commissions connected to the Camargue wetlands. For Milan they made new work inspired by Leonardo da Vinci’s Codex Atlanticus. Both changes belong to a project that has been growing for years. A place can leave behind something as specific as a recording, a sheet of paper or a film fragment, and the next city offers another way to put those pieces together.
