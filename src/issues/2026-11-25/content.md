## 在史密斯菲尔德老市场，伦敦博物馆公布新馆

### 中文

伦敦博物馆 11 月 24 日公布新馆现场，28 日在史密斯菲尔德旧综合市场正式对公众开放。改造花了十年，由 Stanton Williams、Asif Khan Studio 和 Julian Harrap Architects 合作。第一阶段开放的是综合市场，隔壁的家禽市场按馆方计划在 2028 年开放，届时加入临展空间和学习中心。

进馆遇到的是「Our Time」。市场的玻璃顶留在原处，下面摆上了长椅，参观者可以停留再决定往哪走。馆方把叙事分成 Real Time、Our Time 和 Past Time 三部分。公开的展品名单里，罗马马赛克和白金汉宫电话总机同在一馆，东伦敦的 Syd’s 咖啡摊也进了展厅。价钱、电话和日常会面的地方，由此有了各自的实物。

《卫报》的两篇现场评论刚好看出这次改造的难处。Oliver Wainwright 很看重建筑腾出的空间，另一篇展览评论则质疑大堂的尺度与展陈密度。保留市场大厅给了博物馆一处可以直接进来的城市空间。博物馆接下来要回答的问题，在那间宽阔的大厅里看得更清楚：走进来的人，接着能在哪里找到自己熟悉的伦敦？

### English

London Museum unveiled its new home on November 24 and opens it to the public on November 28. The ten-year conversion of Smithfield’s General Market brought together Stanton Williams, Asif Khan Studio and conservation architects Julian Harrap Architects. The adjacent Poultry Market belongs to a later phase. The museum plans to open it in 2028 with temporary exhibition spaces and a learning centre.

Visitors arrive in Our Time. The market’s glass roof still spans the hall, with seats below where people can pause before choosing a direction. The museum divides its displays into Real Time, Our Time and Past Time. Its announced objects include a Roman mosaic, a Buckingham Palace switchboard and Syd’s Coffee Stall from the East End. Trade, phone calls and familiar meeting places each have an object to make them tangible.

Two Guardian reviews reveal the difficulty of the conversion. Oliver Wainwright values the space the architecture makes available. A separate review of the displays questions how much exhibition the scale of the hall leaves room to encounter. Keeping the market hall gives London a civic interior people can walk into. The space also makes a question visible. Where does someone who has just walked in start to recognise their London?

## 首尔《MANA》把维持一件作品的力留在现场

### 中文

首尔一民美术馆的《MANA》11 月 25 日开幕，展至 2027 年 1 月 24 日。馆方借建筑落成百年，请六位艺术家做了一场关于力的展览。开门、搬运、调试这些平时不太进入展览叙事的劳动，成了馆方理解这座建筑如何继续运转的出发点。

Hyungkoo Lee 把钢丝和棱镜吊进中庭。韩媒的现场报道写到，细线的张力维持装置的形态，棱镜让光线随观看位置变化。另一边，Geumhyung Jeong 让身体和自制机械同场。她此前在伦敦 ICA 的《Under Construction》已把雕塑、影像和现场表演摆在一起。来到《MANA》，理解机械的一个办法，是看人怎样反复练习，直到它可以运动。

展览把这些力安排在不同的尺度里。Jisu Kwak 用铁丝、竹和电线搭起结构，Murphy Yum 和 Goyoson 把家用物件与日常记忆带进装置。馆方把阻力和平衡都当成维持形态的条件。顺着这条线索看，作品的边缘也值得停一下。支架、线和调整的痕迹，交代了眼前的东西为何能立在那里。

### English

MANA opened at Ilmin Museum of Art in Seoul on November 25 and runs through January 24, 2027. To mark the centenary of its building, the museum invited six artists to make an exhibition about force. Opening doors, moving things and adjusting equipment formed part of its premise. These are some of the ordinary acts that keep a museum in motion.

Hyungkoo Lee suspends wire and prisms in the atrium. Korean coverage describes how tension holds the work together, while the prisms make light change with the viewer’s position. Geumhyung Jeong brings bodies and handmade machines into the same space. Her earlier *Under Construction* at London’s ICA placed sculpture, video and live performance together. At MANA, watching someone rehearse a machine’s movements provides one way to understand it.

Force appears at different scales. Jisu Kwak builds with wire, bamboo and electrical cable. Murphy Yum and Goyoson work with domestic objects and everyday memories. The museum treats resistance and balance as conditions that let forms hold. That makes the edges worth watching. A bracket, a wire or a trace of adjustment can tell you what lets an object stand.

## 东京今天挂起 260 张关于「Harmony」的海报

看这一展可以先找每张海报上的「Harmony」。有的把它放在视线正中央，有的可能要走到跟前再找。以同样的尺寸装下相异的答案，才显出这道题有多具体。11 月 29 日还有颁奖和评审对谈，想比较评审怎样看纸面的人可以留意这一场。

### English

JAGDA screened work online before judging physical posters. Standing back from a sheet and enlarging it on a screen produce different relationships between type and image. One way into the exhibition is to look for “Harmony” on each poster. It may sit in the middle of your field of view or ask you to come closer. The shared sheet size makes those different answers comparable. The award ceremony and juror talk are scheduled for November 29.

## Richter 把从画布上分出来的纹样放上了墙

### 中文

多哈伊斯兰艺术博物馆外的 MIA Park 新添了一座六边形展亭。卡塔尔博物馆委托 Gerhard Richter 创作这座永久装置，由他与 Selldorf Architects 合作完成。公园里的第一眼先看到外墙的彩色纹样，绕到入口，才会遇到里面的条纹画、镜面作品和天窗下的条纹塔。

纹样来自 Richter 对自己一幅 1990 年抽象画的处理。他把画面切分，沿切线做镜像与重复，最终形成密集的色带。Serpentine 介绍过近亲作品《Strip-Tower》的同一处理方法。多哈展亭把这项工作推到墙面上。站得远，条带像建筑的表皮。沿外墙走近，细密的色块才逐渐分开。

展亭随首届 Rubaiya Qatar 的开幕在 11 月下旬亮相。后者有自己的展期，展亭作为永久委托将留在公园。把照片和建筑师的平面描述一起看，更能理解 Richter 为什么把一组源于画布的纹样放在户外。以前需要沿画面移动的视线，现在可以带着身体走完一圈。

### English

A hexagonal pavilion has joined MIA Park outside Doha’s Museum of Islamic Art. Commissioned by Qatar Museums as a permanent work, it was designed by Gerhard Richter in collaboration with Selldorf Architects. From the park, the exterior patterns come into view first. Inside are Strip paintings, mirror works and a Strip Tower beneath the central opening.

The pattern originated in a process Richter applied to a 1990 abstract painting. He divided the image and used mirroring and repetition to make dense bands of colour. Serpentine documented a related process for its *Strip-Tower*. The Doha pavilion takes that work out onto a wall. From a distance the strips read as a building skin. As the viewer approaches, smaller blocks of colour become distinct.

The pavilion arrived in late November alongside the inaugural Rubaiya Qatar. The quadrennial has its own closing date, while the permanent commission is intended to remain in the park. Read the photographs alongside the architect’s account of the floor plan. Richter has given a pattern that once directed the eye across a painting a space the viewer can circle on foot.

「设计@HKG」两周活动在 11 月 24 日收束。开场装置《BREATHE WITH ME—SOFT RESET》由 Annabelle Schneider 创作，地点在香港山顶广场。主办方请观众免费进场，把开放时段和少数因活动临时关闭的时间一并写在页面上。从商场动线走进去，展览安排给观众的动作很简单，先停一会儿。

Schneider 把这次装置写成一次放慢的邀请。她的项目页记录了柔软结构与感官设计。香港设计中心这份节目单则把同一周的作品排进街道、社区、店铺与公共交通。这个安排让两种读法同时成立。参加者可以走完几段设计路线，也可以为一个装置多留十分钟。

这类展览放进商业空间有一处实际的好处。赶路的人本来就会经过入口，停一会儿也不用先买票或理解一篇策展导言。活动结束后值得从设计师的现场记录回看这一点，作品为途中的人留出了什么样的停留空间。

### English

DESIGN@HKG’s two-week programme ended on November 24. Annabelle Schneider’s opening installation, *BREATHE WITH ME—SOFT RESET*, took place in Hong Kong’s Peak Galleria. Admission was free. The organisers listed both its public hours and the short periods when other events occupied the room. The gesture it asked of someone entering from the shops was straightforward. Pause here for a while.

Schneider presents the work as an invitation to slow down. Her project page records the soft structures and sensory design. The wider Hong Kong Design Centre programme placed work across streets, neighbourhoods, shops and public transport. A visitor could follow a design route across several stops or give one installation an extra ten minutes.

There is a practical advantage to placing such a work in a commercial space. Someone on the way elsewhere already passes the door, and pausing requires neither a ticket nor time to read a curatorial introduction. The artist’s documentation leaves one question worth returning to after the programme has ended. What kind of room did the installation make for a person who was already on their way somewhere?
