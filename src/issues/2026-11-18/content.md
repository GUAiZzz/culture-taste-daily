# 给物件留一点距离

ROOM BETWEEN THINGS

香港的设计展把衣服和家具摆进同一个展馆，伦敦的模型要走近了才能听见，唱片也给两部电影的声音留出单独播放的时间。今天从东西摆在哪里、怎么被看见和听见开始，追到它原先待过的房间。

## 在香港会展，把衣服和家具放回同一间房

### 中文

11 月 18 日，DesignInspire 在香港会议展览中心开幕。今天是业界日，公众从 19 日到 21 日可以免费进场。意大利展馆 ABITO/HK 把过去一个世纪的服装与家具放在一起。看完一件衣服，可以接着看它同时代的人怎样布置自己的家。

在意大利语里，abito 可以指一件衣服，也可以是「我住在」这个动词形式。Palomba Serafini Associati 用这组词义安排了最初的米兰展览，把穿着和居住放进同一段设计史。米兰的陈列曾把 Laura Aponte 1967 年的服装与 Gaetano Pesce 1969 年设计的 Up 并置。项目后来去过新加坡。香港站由意大利外交与国际合作部与米兰国际家具展共同推动，加入了本地的设计线索。

展场里还有瑞士团队 atelier oï 的《Breathing in Motion》。它和 ABITO/HK 提供的是两种观看方式：一个让人沿着衣服、家具和年代辨认变化，一个用会动的装置，让人停下来观察材料怎样改变形态。两项展出同在一处，却适合用不同的速度看。

如果去香港会展，可以给 ABITO/HK 留点时间，挑一组并置的服装和家具比较。它们可能跟着不同的生活节奏变化。两地各自的材料、制作方法和使用方式，比展馆之间相似的表面更值得看。

### English

DesignInspire opens at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre on November 18. The first day is reserved for the trade, with free public admission from November 19 through 21. The Italian pavilion ABITO/HK brings a century of clothing and furniture into the same exhibition. After looking at a garment, you can see how people of the same period furnished a home.

In Italian, abito can mean a garment or the verb form “I live.” Palomba Serafini Associati used the relationship to organize the original Milan exhibition, placing dress and domestic life on one timeline. In Milan, a 1967 garment by Laura Aponte was displayed with Gaetano Pesce’s 1969 Up design. The project subsequently traveled to Singapore. Italy’s foreign ministry and Salone del Mobile.Milano helped bring it to Hong Kong with a local contribution.

The fair also presents Breathing in Motion by the Swiss studio atelier oï. The two projects ask for different kinds of attention. In one, garments, furniture and dates lead you through changes over time. In the other, a moving installation gives you time to watch material change shape.

At ABITO/HK, spend a few minutes with one pairing. A garment and a piece of furniture might keep different rhythms of everyday life. Materials, methods and uses can tell you more about each place than a resemblance between two displays.

## Yuri Suzuki 把游乐场缩成可以听的模型

### 中文

伦敦伊斯灵顿的 Osh Gallery 展出 Yuri Suzuki 的《Onomatopia》。艺术家把游乐场和世博会的建筑缩成几组模型，在里面留下号角、转轮和可以操作的部件。有些声音要等观众动手才出现。看展的路线因此也有了顺序，先找到声音从哪来，再回头找驱动它的那件东西。

2018 年，他把《Sonic Playground》装到亚特兰大 High Museum 的露天广场，用彩色声学装置传递和改变人声。在公共广场，另一个人可以站在出声的一端；缩小进室内以后，模型把看不见的传声关系摆到了更近的位置。手能操作，眼睛也能追到物件之间的距离。

Suzuki 和日本 E&Y 做的 Ambient Machine 又是另一种尺度。32 个开关让使用者给房间组合背景声，Japan House London 曾把它放进展厅让人试用。在这台机器里，人可以先选声音，之后退开去做别的事。《Onomatopia》把注意力留在装置旁边，下一次转动会不会发出和刚才一样的声音，得亲手试。

展览由艺术家与策展人合作完成。旧邮政分拣所里的模型和广场上的装置也给了一个可见的差别。同一种转动和传声，放进公共空间时可以连接陌生人，缩成展品时，人先有机会弄清楚它怎么工作。

### English

Yuri Suzuki’s Onomatopia is on view at Osh Gallery in Islington. The artist scales down the architecture of amusement parks and world fairs into models with horns, wheels and pieces you can operate. Some sounds begin only when a visitor does something. You can follow the sound back to the object that generated it.

In 2018, Suzuki put Sonic Playground on the High Museum of Art’s outdoor plaza in Atlanta. Its colorful acoustic sculptures transmitted and modified voices. On a public square another person can stand at the far end of a sound. At the scale of a gallery model, you can use your hand and follow the shorter distance between components with your eyes.

The Ambient Machine, developed with Japan’s E&Y, works on another scale. Its 32 switches let a listener assemble background sounds for a room, and Japan House London previously offered visitors a chance to try it. A listener can choose sounds and then go back to something else. At Onomatopia the object keeps your attention, because another turn might produce a different sound.

The models at the former sorting house and the sculptures on the plaza suggest different uses for the same basic acts of turning and transmitting. In a public space they can connect strangers. At model scale there is time to see how the connection works.

## Kobe 9 回归，鞋底里还有菲律宾的人字拖

### 中文

11 月 18 日是美国多家球鞋媒体报道的 SNKRS 发售日。Nike 菲律宾的页面列出了同款 Kobe 9 EM Low Protro「Laser Crimson and Obsidian」的产品资料，日本渠道报道的则是 11 月 27 日。这是 2014 年菲律宾限定 Kobe 9 的回归。鞋面重新用了参考传统编织席 banig 的图案，半透明外底里还放了 tsinelas，也就是当地常见的人字拖图形。

Nike 还把金属光泽的细节指向 jeepney。三个线索各有位置，鞋面在远一点的地方就能认出来，外底图案要把鞋翻过来才能看到。人字拖放在专门做来打球的鞋下方，这个位置很具体。它来自品牌对当地球场日常的描述，也给了今天拿起鞋的人一个可以追问的细节。

2014 年的报道已经在讲 banig 和 tsinelas，它们有原版可对照。Nike 把新鞋列为 Protro，描述了工程网布与新的缓震配置；这些是本次产品的技术说法。编织花纹也有边界：它印在鞋面上，不能把这双鞋理解成按 banig 工艺手工编织。

看这一版，值得把 2014 年的图片和今天的产品照翻到同一个角度。哪些地方保留了当时的讲法，哪些归于新结构，可以直接从鞋上找答案。

### English

Several US sneaker publications report a November 18 SNKRS release. Nike Philippines provides product details for the matching Kobe 9 EM Low Protro in Laser Crimson and Obsidian, while Japanese coverage lists November 27. The shoe revisits a 2014 Kobe 9 limited release for the Philippines. The upper carries a pattern inspired by banig, traditional woven mats, and the translucent outsole contains a graphic of a tsinelas, a familiar flip-flop.

Nike also connects the metallic details to jeepneys. The references occupy different places. You can recognize the pattern on the upper from a distance, but you have to turn the shoe over to see the flip-flop. Putting that everyday object under a basketball shoe gives Nike’s account of local court life a specific detail readers can examine.

Reports from 2014 already described the banig and tsinelas references, so there is an original to compare. Nike describes this Protro edition in terms of engineered mesh and an updated cushioning configuration. Those are the company’s product claims. The printed upper evokes weaving without being a handwoven banig.

Put a photograph of the 2014 shoe beside today’s product image at the same angle. You can see which references survived and which features belong to the new construction.

## Liz Harris 让两部水的电影共用一张唱片

### 中文

Liz Harris 把两部短片的配乐合成《Music For Two Films About Water》，由 Kranky 安排在 11 月 27 日发行。官方 Bandcamp 现在有《Constellation》的一首配乐可听。《We Exhale》那一首随整张唱片发行。两部电影都从水出发，各自给声音留了不同的工作。

Tanja Engelberts 的《We Exhale》看的是罗讷河怎样经过筑坝、工业化和污染改变。牧野贵的《Constellation》在水的纹理里叠放影像片段，关心记忆和感知。前一部让声音面对一条有地理位置的河，后一部里的水可以让不同时间留下的画面浮出来。

资料列出的配乐材料包括 Mellotron、Wurlitzer、磁带循环和现场录音。Harris 以前长期与实验影像作者 Paul Clipson 合作，这次以本名推出配乐，跟 Grouper 的艺名区分开。在唱片单独播出时，原先贴着影像出现的声音也可以离开镜头，留下长度、重复和间隔给人听。

现在适合先听已经公开的那一面。听完再回到短片资料，把影片里需要看见的东西和单听配乐时会留意的东西分开。两首放在一起的效果，要等 27 日。

### English

Liz Harris has brought the scores for two short films together as Music For Two Films About Water, scheduled for November 27 on Kranky. The score from Constellation is available now on the official Bandcamp page. The piece from We Exhale follows with the full record. Both films begin with water and give the music different work to do.

Tanja Engelberts’s We Exhale follows the Rhône through changes brought by dams, industry and pollution. Takashi Makino’s Constellation layers fragments of images amid textures of water and considers memory and perception. In one film the sounds answer to a geographically specific river. In the other, water is a place for images from different times to surface.

The listed materials include Mellotron, Wurlitzer, tape loops and field recordings. Harris worked for years with experimental filmmaker Paul Clipson. She is releasing these scores under her own name, separate from the Grouper alias. Played without a screen, sound that once accompanied an image can be heard through duration, repetition and interval.

The released piece is available to hear now. After listening, return to the film descriptions and consider what each medium lets you notice. Hearing how the two pieces work as a pair will have to wait until November 27.

## 布朗库西的雕塑，连同底座与工作室一起看

### 中文

纽约 MoMA 把布朗库西的雕塑、底座、工具、家具和他拍的照片摆进同一场展览。《Brancusi: The Artist and His Studio》从 10 月 25 日展至 2027 年 2 月 27 日。作品从巴黎工作室的收藏里借来，策展也把它们在工作室里彼此相邻的关系带进了博物馆。

从 1916 年起，布朗库西长期在巴黎 Ronsin 巷工作、居住和接待访客。底座能被移动，作品可以叠放、分开，再重新拍摄。看《无尽之柱》的单件介绍是一种经验。看艺术家在玻璃底片里让几根柱子、《鱼》和《丽达》同处一室，可以观察它们的高度、间隔和朝向怎样一起成立。

1957 年，布朗库西把工作室的整体内容遗赠给法国。蓬皮杜中心的巴黎场地翻修期间，柏林新国家美术馆今年先呈现了工作室的局部重构，纽约的展览现在继续用工作室作为入口。一个空间可以被搬到不同的博物馆，作品之间的关系却需要每次重新安排。

在 MoMA 不妨先认一件作品，再到档案照片里找它。照片里如果连台座和旁边的物件都能看清楚，雕塑在展厅里显得孤立的那一面就有了参照。

### English

MoMA brings Brancusi’s sculptures together with bases, tools, furniture and the photographs he made himself. Brancusi: The Artist and His Studio runs from October 25 through February 27, 2027. The Paris studio collection supplies loans, and the exhibition also brings into view relationships between objects that once stood beside each other.

From 1916, Brancusi lived, worked and received visitors in his studio on the impasse Ronsin in Paris. Bases could move. Works could be stacked, separated and photographed again. An individual Endless Column is one encounter. In a glass negative that places several columns beside Fish and Leda, you can also see how their heights, intervals and orientations work together.

Brancusi bequeathed the studio contents to France in 1957. During the Centre Pompidou’s renovations, Berlin’s Neue Nationalgalerie presented a partial reconstruction earlier this year. The New York exhibition now makes the studio its point of entry. A collection can travel, but the relationships between its works need to be arranged at each stop.

At MoMA, pick one work and look for it in the archival photographs. If you can see the base and the objects alongside it, you have a point of comparison for the sculpture as it stands in the gallery.

亚特兰大 SCAD FASH 的《Emily in Paris: Fashion Without Rules》从 11 月 11 日展至明年 3 月 14 日。展览以 Marylin Fitoussi 的剧集服装为主，摆进超过 100 套造型。第一季的红色贝雷帽常被截成一张「巴黎」的图。在展厅里，它可以和黑白格纹的整套衣服放回一起看。

电视剧的服装有个实际限制：观众有时很快就要认出谁进了房间。Emily 的高对比颜色和叠搭、Sylvie 的利落剪裁、Mindy 的表演服，在同一个镜头里分得很清楚。Fitoussi 在采访里讲过，场景进入意大利后，Emily 的造型也跟着调整。把几季的服装相邻摆放，正好可以看这些习惯如何留下，又如何变了。

Versailles 的 Marie Antoinette 灵感造型可以和系列里其他衣服并置。草图和制作资料也让人看到借来的、改过的和专门做的东西怎样进了同一个镜头。服装在展厅里停下来，给剪裁和配饰多留了几秒钟。

### English

Emily in Paris: Fashion Without Rules runs at Atlanta’s SCAD FASH from November 11 through March 14, 2027. The show gathers more than 100 ensembles designed for the Netflix series by Marylin Fitoussi. The first season’s red beret often circulates as a single image of “Paris.” In the gallery you can put it back with the rest of the black-and-white gingham outfit.

Television costumes have a practical job. Sometimes viewers need to know quickly who has entered the room. Emily’s high contrast and layering, Sylvie’s sharp tailoring and Mindy’s performance clothes distinguish the characters in a shot. Fitoussi has discussed adjusting Emily’s wardrobe as the setting moved to Italy. Putting seasons side by side lets you see which habits persist and which change.

The exhibition brings the Versailles fashions inspired by Marie Antoinette into view alongside other outfits. Sketches and production materials give some sense of how borrowed, altered and purpose-made pieces can appear in the same shot. A static display leaves a few more seconds for the cut, fabric and accessories.

SCAD is also exhibiting C.Z. Guest’s wardrobe. One room shows how a crew dressed a fictional person. The other follows a real person’s choices over time. Each gives you a different way to read a set of clothes.

### English

Yoshitomo Nara’s first solo exhibition with David Zwirner spans two Chelsea galleries from November 5 through December 12. The gallery also published a playlist selected by Nara. It stands on its own, and it gives you another route back into the exhibition after looking at the paintings.

## Tim Reaper 从一支行进乐队打开个人专辑

### 中文

伦敦 jungle 制作人 Tim Reaper 公布了第一张个人专辑《World’s End》，由 Hyperdub 安排在 2027 年 2 月 19 日发行。现在可以听开场曲《Roll Call》。它用美国行进乐队采样和 footwork 的节拍开门，给下一首会去哪里留了空间。

Tim Reaper 在这张专辑之前已经做了多年单曲、EP 和合作唱片。2024 年他和 Kloke 在 Hyperdub 发过《In Full Effect》。他也提到《Scorched Earth》的创作背景：早先的一次谈话，让他开始检视自己能不能继续试别的节奏。新专辑的介绍列出了 gqom、footwork、drill 和 techno 等线索。

这种写法容易让人先数曲风。现在更具体的入口是听《Roll Call》里行进乐队采样怎样被拆开，再从 footwork 的拍点判断它怎么重新落地。采样的出处和拼接后的速度，提供了不会被同一个风格名概括掉的细节。

完整专辑目前还没有释出。如果想先认识制作人的节奏取向，可以接着找他与 Kloke 的唱片，或者那套长篇 RA 混音。把旧作和已经发布的单曲分别听过，再回来判断新专辑实际走了多远。

### English

London jungle producer Tim Reaper has announced his first solo album, World’s End, for February 19, 2027 on Hyperdub. The opener, Roll Call, is available now. American marching-band samples meet a footwork rhythm and leave room for the next track to go somewhere else.

The record follows years of singles, EPs and collaborations. Reaper and Kloke released In Full Effect on Hyperdub in 2024. Reaper has also described how a conversation that led to his Scorched Earth EP made him examine what he could do with other rhythms. The new album’s notes identify gqom, footwork, drill and techno among its points of reference.

A list of genres can be a distracting way to begin. The available single offers something more exact. Follow how the marching-band sample is cut, then listen for the places it lands against the footwork beat. Where a sound came from and what happens at the new speed are details a single genre label cannot carry.

While the full album is forthcoming, there is time to hear the record with Kloke or Reaper’s long mix for Resident Advisor. Those older choices and the new single give you something to compare when the album arrives.

## Salman Toor 借一幅旧画重新安排酒吧里的目光

### 中文

伦敦 Courtauld Gallery 正在展出 Salman Toor 的《Someone Like You》，展期从 10 月 2 日到 2027 年 1 月 10 日。19 幅画里有人在酒吧、车里，也有人在朋友之间。Toor 用自己的记忆提供人物关系，再让他们生活在虚构场景里。

《The Bar on East 13th》里有一层可以辨认的美术馆关系。Courtauld 收藏马奈的《女神游乐厅的吧台》，评论者也把两幅画放在一起看。Toor 画的是纽约酒吧。他借用后来观者熟悉的画面关系，让朋友、酒客和看画的人重新占据其中的位置。

Whitney 2020 年的 Toor 个展已经关心室内、亲密感以及被他人目光打断的生活。《Someone Like You》把这种关系放进整批不同尺度的作品里。像《Ghost Ball》这样的大幅画增加了人和动作的数量，较小的车内、房间画面把视线收近。有些人物紧挨着，有些人隔着一个画框的距离看起来更孤单。

艺术家说过，画中的酷儿和移民生活构成了亲密与暴露之间的张力。这组画留给观众一个很具体的动作：看场面里谁能看向谁，以及自己站在画外时，究竟在看谁。

### English

Salman Toor’s Someone Like You is on view at London’s Courtauld Gallery from October 2 until January 10, 2027. Its 19 paintings put people in bars, cars and rooms with friends. Toor draws on memory for relationships and gives the figures fictional settings of their own.

The Bar on East 13th has a connection to the gallery’s collection. The Courtauld owns Manet’s A Bar at the Folies-Bergère, and critics have considered the two pictures together. Toor sets his scene in a New York bar. The arrangement gives friends, drinkers and the viewer different places in a visual relationship familiar from the older painting.

Toor’s Whitney exhibition in 2020 already explored what happens when intimacy and domestic space meet another person’s gaze. At the Courtauld those relationships play out across works of very different sizes. A large picture such as Ghost Ball has room for more people and movement. Smaller cars and rooms pull your view in close. Proximity changes the way a group reads.

Toor has described queer and immigrant lives in terms of a tension between intimacy and exposure. The paintings make it possible to look for something specific: who can see whom in the scene, and who we are looking at from outside the frame.

### 中文

CDG 在东京涩谷 PARCO 一楼开了 18 天的限时店，到 11 月 17 日结束。和 Stüssy 做的联名在 7 日先行推出。活动页显示，同一个店面还放了 CDG 与 Vans、Alpha Industries 的项目。PARCO 的更新计划把 CDG 常设店安排在 11 月 20 日，于二楼开张。

日本媒体的这轮图像把 SS Link、Surfman 和 World Tour 放到 CDG 的字样旁边。先行款报道包括外套、卫衣、水洗长袖以及帽子、包和围巾等。把图案放进围巾或包上，看到它们的时间和角度跟衣服不一样，标志因此有了几种具体的去处。

Stüssy 在 2020 年四十周年时也曾和 CDG 合作。前一次官方资料把徽标放在 MA-1、条纹夹克、长裤与 T 恤上，销售经过部分 Chapter 店、Dover Street Market 和品牌网站。这次涩谷先行发售的到店顺序在首两天通过抽签安排。已有报道确证的是这次东京现场的规则。商品之后在哪些市场出现，仍要查当地官方通知。

快闪店昨天结束。回头看这批联名，可以把 2020 年的夹克和这次的包、围巾放在一张图里。两个标志很久以前就见过面，今天值得看的是它们各自离开衣服以后，还能跟着什么东西出现在街上。

### English

CDG’s temporary store on the first floor of Shibuya PARCO ran through November 17. The Stüssy collaboration went on presale on November 7. The venue’s listing places other CDG collaborations, including projects with Vans and Alpha Industries, in the same shop. PARCO plans to open a permanent CDG shop on the second floor on November 20.

Japanese coverage shows SS Link, Surfman and World Tour graphics alongside CDG lettering. The reported presale range included jackets, hoodies, long-sleeve shirts, hats, bags and scarves. A graphic on a scarf or bag surfaces at a different angle and moment from the same graphic on a garment.

Stüssy previously collaborated with CDG for its 40th anniversary in 2020. The official archive shows branding on an MA-1, pinstripe jackets and pants, and T-shirts. That release used selected Chapter stores, Dover Street Market and Stüssy’s website. For the first two days of the Shibuya presale, a lottery determined entry order. The documented rule applies to that Tokyo event. Later availability in other markets needs its own official notice.

The shop closed yesterday. The old jacket and the newer bags and scarves make a useful comparison. The logos already had a history together. The more interesting change is what they can travel on after leaving the clothes.
