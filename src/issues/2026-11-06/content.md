# 物件里的生活

MATERIALS IN EVERYDAY LIFE

一双鞋、一张芦苇长凳、一套现场录音、一扇窗和一桌毛线食物，各自带出人的生活。今天选了十二篇，走进设计、衣服、音乐与展览。

11 月 6 日的新发行、已经开幕的展览，以及上海明天的两场新展，各自保留准确日期。正文依据公开资料与访谈，评论和创作主张保留出处。

## 一个展览，怎样展开 Black design 里的差异？

### 中文

伦敦设计博物馆的《The Nue Black Aesthetic》于 11 月 6 日启幕，展至 2027 年 8 月 8 日。Charlene Prempeh 担任客座策展人，六个章节汇集超过一百件作品，涵盖时装、家具、建筑、产品设计与雕塑。博物馆列出的十六位设计师，包括 Samuel Ross、Bianca Saunders、Feben、Ini Archibong 与 Giles Tettey Nartey。 [Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/exhibitions/the-nue-black-aesthetic)

共同身份怎样成为入口，又让各自的经历继续清晰，是这个展览提出的问题。标题里的 Nue 指向 nuance；展方希望观众看见，Black design 并不对应一种固定的材料或外形。官方作品资料里，编辫摇椅、陶器、服装与住宅并置，已经给出了不同的进入方式。

Prempeh 在 Wallpaper* 的访谈里强调，不同非洲与加勒比地区有各自的历史。她的策展判断需要通过展厅里的作品关系得到检验。可以沿着材料、结构与用途逐件读，再看共同标题究竟增加了哪些联系。

作为背景，《The Missing Thread》曾在 Somerset House 把 Black British fashion 放进家庭、剪裁、表演和夜生活；Princeton 的《Hold》则在另一种艺术语境里处理 Black aesthetics。它们各有范围，不能相互替代。设计师自己的访谈还能把大题目落回具体实践：Giles Tettey Nartey 谈 Accra 与 London、记忆与日常仪式，Mac Collins 谈家具与牙买加 heritage。把这些差异带回作品，才能继续讨论设计师怎样进入收藏、出版与设计史。

这里依据公开材料提出阅读角度，尚不把策展目标写成已经实现的现场效果。

### English

The Nue Black Aesthetic opens at London’s Design Museum on 6 November and runs until 8 August 2027. Guest-curated by Charlene Prempeh, its six sections bring together more than 100 objects across fashion, furniture, architecture, product design and sculpture. The museum names sixteen designers, including Samuel Ross, Bianca Saunders, Feben, Ini Archibong and Giles Tettey Nartey. [Design Museum](https://designmuseum.org/exhibitions/the-nue-black-aesthetic)

The exhibition asks how a shared identity can provide an entrance while keeping individual histories visible. “Nue” suggests nuance. The official selection includes a braided rocking chair, ceramics, clothing and a house: different materials and uses already offer different ways into the subject.

In her Wallpaper* interview, Prempeh stresses the distinct histories of African and Caribbean communities. That curatorial proposition still needs to be tested against the works’ actual relationships in the galleries. Following material, structure and use offers one way to see what connections the shared title adds.

Historical references have their own boundaries. Somerset House’s The Missing Thread considered Black British fashion through home, tailoring, performance and nightlife; Princeton’s Hold addressed Black aesthetics in a different artistic setting. Neither substitutes for the present exhibition. Designers’ interviews make the question more specific: Nartey discusses Accra and London, memory and everyday ritual, while Mac Collins connects furniture with Jamaican heritage. Bringing those distinctions back to the objects can open a further discussion about collections, publishing and design history.

This is a reading of published material, not a firsthand assessment of whether the exhibition achieves its aims.

## Kith 把纽约的一整天装进冬日衣橱

### 中文

Kith Women Winter 2026 于 11 月 6 日按各市场当地时间发售。《Echoes of the City》在曼哈顿旧 Bank of Manhattan 拍摄，系列包括外套、针织、晚间着装、新手袋 Katarina，以及与 adidas Originals 合作的 Stan Smith Suede Ballet。品牌把一天里办事、下班喝酒和去朋友聚会的场景连在一起。 [Kith](https://kith.com/blogs/discover/kith-women-winter-2026-echoes-of-the-city-lookbook)

Stan Smith Ballet 值得单独看。低矮鞋身、Mary Jane 式横带与棕色系麂皮，让这个源于网球鞋的名字进入另一种穿着姿态。adidas 的历史资料追溯到 Robert Haillet 与后来的 Stan Smith；今天的合作保留名字与部分识别细节，却改变了鞋身和脚背的关系。Footwear Magazine 也把这次材质与轮廓的搭配作为观察重点。

舞鞋进入日常衣橱有更长的历史。FIT 的《Ballerina: Fashion’s Modern Muse》讨论过芭蕾对服装与平底鞋的影响；English National Ballet 则把软底舞鞋与足尖鞋的结构和用途分开介绍。这里的生活方式鞋借用了舞蹈的视觉语汇，不能因此当作专业舞鞋，也不能凭照片判断长时间步行的舒适度。

Kith 对女装生活场景的描述，也有自己的零售背景。2023 年的 Vogue 访谈里，Ronnie Fieg 谈过女装身份与门店体验；2024 年的空间报道记录了旗舰店里的花店和咖啡区域。今年 Lisa 担任春季客座设计师，是另一项已公布的合作，不能据此推断她参与了本次冬装。把这些时期分开，才看得清今天这套衣橱怎样延续、又怎样调整品牌对女性日常的想象。

实际穿着仍是下一层检验：外套怎样应对天气，鞋怎样适应步行，这些都要离开 Lookbook 才有答案。

A 2024 interior report documented the flagship’s café and flower shop. Lisa’s published role as guest designer for Spring 2026 belongs to another collection and does not establish involvement in this winter release. Keeping those periods separate helps us examine how the present wardrobe extends or adjusts the brand’s imagined daily life.

Weather and walking will provide tests beyond the campaign: what the coat and shoes feel like in use still requires wearing them.

## 一件羽绒服，借了军大衣的骨架

### 中文

Saturdays NYC 与 Marmot 的首次合作羽绒派克于 11 月 6 日在日本发售，以 Dima Down Parka Pro 为基础，借用 M-47 OVERCOAT 的腰带、前口袋与门襟细节。JUN 公告列出 PERTEX SHIELD PRO 面料、750 蓬松度羽绒、两组内外配色，以及 66,000 日元的日本含税售价。 [JUN / PR Times](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001509.000006099.html)

判断这类合作，可以把标志暂时放到一边，检查衣服的变化落在哪里。腰带影响体积怎样围住身体，口袋与门襟组织正面，炭灰配酒红、米色配浅蓝，则在敞开时呈现另一层颜色。它们都与穿衣动作有关。

材料数字需要分开读。PERTEX 将 Shield Pro 描述为采用微孔膜的防水透湿面料；这是供应商对技术的说明。REI 的羽绒指南提醒，fill power 衡量羽绒的蓬松效率，衣服的实际保暖还与充绒量、分布、外层和穿着条件有关。750 不能单独换算成适用温度，也不是这件衣服的实测成绩。

两家品牌的来路给这种重组提供了背景。Saturdays 创始人在早年的不同访谈里，把纽约工作生活、冲浪、咖啡与社区空间连在一起，也讨论过进入日本时怎样保留品牌身份。Marmot 创始人在 UC Santa Cruz 的回顾里，则谈到攀登经验、缝制睡袋和耐用装备的起点。今天这件衣服把两条来路放到同一个轮廓里；是否成立，可以继续检查剪裁、活动余量与长期使用。

### English

Saturdays NYC and Marmot release their first collaborative down parka in Japan on 6 November. Based on the Dima Down Parka Pro, it borrows a belt, front pockets and placket details from the M-47 OVERCOAT. JUN lists PERTEX SHIELD PRO fabric, 750-fill-power down, two contrasting shell-and-lining combinations and a Japanese tax-inclusive price of ¥66,000. [JUN / PR Times](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001509.000006099.html)

Setting the logos aside makes the alterations easier to inspect. The belt organizes volume around the body; pockets and the placket order the front. Charcoal with burgundy and beige with light blue reveal a second colour when the coat opens. Each change belongs to an action of wearing.

The technical numbers need distinct readings. PERTEX describes Shield Pro as waterproof-breathable fabric using a microporous membrane; that is the supplier’s account. REI’s down guide explains that fill power measures loft efficiency. Warmth also depends on fill quantity and distribution, the shell, other layers and conditions. The number 750 alone provides neither a temperature rating nor an independent test of this garment.

The brands’ earlier accounts supply context. In separate interviews, Saturdays’ founders connected New York working life with surfing, coffee and gathering places, and discussed retaining identity as they entered Japan. Marmot’s founders, recalling their beginnings for UC Santa Cruz, described climbing, sewing sleeping bags and seeking durable equipment. The new parka places those histories within one silhouette. Cut, movement and long-term use remain the useful next tests.

## 39 张 CD，把 Bob Dylan 的巡演重新排开

今天可以从 Fort Worth 进入，再找另一场的同一首歌。39 张唱片提供的是比较空间，听完全部并不需要成为第一步。这篇介绍依据档案与报道，尚不冒充整套录音的亲听乐评。

Fort Worth can be an entrance, followed by the same song at another show. The set offers room for comparison; finishing all 39 discs need not be the first task. This introduction follows published archives and reporting rather than posing as a firsthand review of the complete recordings.

## 一尊印度象牙像抵达庞贝之后

### 中文

Fondazione Prada 的《Global Antiquity》于 11 月 5 日在米兰开幕，展至 2027 年 3 月 1 日。超过 150 件作品与器物，把约公元前 600 年至公元 1000 年的跨区域联系带进展厅。一尊在公元 79 年火山喷发前已抵达庞贝的印度次大陆女性象牙像，是展方举出的例子之一。 [Fondazione Prada](https://www.fondazioneprada.org/project/global-antiquity/?lang=en)

这件小像的身份长期受到讨论。纽约大学的研究页面记录了「印度维纳斯」「庞贝拉克什米」等称谓，也并列了对来源、形象与使用语境的不同解释。米兰大学 LANX 的论文继续把它的主体、来源与用途当作研究问题。把不确定性留在物件旁边，能避免一个熟悉的名字过早结束讨论。

展方提出，发现地点与制作地点同样重要。大都会博物馆梳理的陆路、海路与商埠，让两点之间出现中间环节；关于东方商品在罗马社会流通的研究，则把季风、运输与规模估算的争论带进来。路线不能只凭一件器物倒推。

材料辨认本身也会变化。Roberta Tomber 对陶罐的研究指出，部分曾被报告为罗马器物的遗存，实际与美索不达米亚的鱼雷罐有关；另一篇关于印度 Junnar 雕像的论文，把「可能是 Eros」与身份仍不确定放在一起。Rebecca Darley 的研究综述进一步讨论怎样让罗马离开印度洋叙事的唯一中心。

Rem Koolhaas 与 AMO/OMA 的展陈将这些故事组织为交汇的关系。可以沿着材料、图像与地点看下去，同时保留每件物品尚不能交代的路线、主人与意义。

### English

Global Antiquity opened at Fondazione Prada in Milan on 5 November and runs until 1 March 2027. More than 150 works and artifacts explore cross-regional relations from roughly 600 BCE to 1000 CE. Among the foundation’s examples is an ivory female figure from the Indian subcontinent that reached Pompeii before the eruption of 79 CE. [Fondazione Prada](https://www.fondazioneprada.org/project/global-antiquity/?lang=en)

The small figure’s identity remains debated. A New York University research page records names including “Indian Venus” and “Pompeii Lakshmi,” alongside different readings of origin, imagery and use. A paper in the University of Milan’s LANX continues to treat those questions as research problems. Keeping uncertainty beside the object prevents a familiar label from prematurely ending the inquiry.

The foundation argues that where an artifact was found matters alongside where it was made. The Metropolitan Museum’s account of land routes, sea routes and trading ports supplies intermediate stages. Research on eastern goods in Roman society adds monsoon winds, transport and disputed estimates of scale. A route cannot be reconstructed from one object alone.

Material identifications can change too. Roberta Tomber’s study identifies some vessels once reported as Roman as Mesopotamian torpedo jars. A paper on a figurine found at Junnar places a possible identification as Eros beside continuing uncertainty. Rebecca Darley’s review asks how Rome can cease to be the sole centre of Indian Ocean narratives.

Rem Koolhaas and AMO/OMA organize the exhibition through intersecting stories. Following materials, imagery and locations also means retaining what an object cannot yet tell us about its route, owner or meaning.

### English
