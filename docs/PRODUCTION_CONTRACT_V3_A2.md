## Amended rule

Every selected main story must resolve and verify at least one story-specific image from a first-party official source before the issue is locked. A first-party official source is the subject, brand, designer, artist, institution, venue, organizer, or another directly responsible canonical public channel for the exact event or object.

An independent publication, search result, discovery index, repost, social mirror, stock library, or generated image cannot satisfy this gate. An owned diagram, illustration, or text-led composition may supplement the official image, but it cannot replace the official-image evidence required for story selection.

The public source chain must identify the official origin page. The private ledger must separately record the exact image, verification time, official-source relationship, credit, documentary or contextual role, and usage-rights basis. A contextual official image must be labeled as contextual and must not be presented as current documentary evidence.

Official provenance is not publication permission. An official page, direct asset URL, credit, press area, or embeddable image does not itself grant reuse rights. The image may be copied into a production artifact only when the existing V3 rights gate has a documented applicable basis. When rights remain unknown, a visibly non-production Preview may use the existing linked external-image treatment only when it opens the official origin page and remains `BLOCKED` from production. The production artifact and archival edition must not depend on that external image.

If no accurate official image can be verified, hold or omit the story. Do not fill the slot with a media image, search-result image, AI image, or invented documentary visual merely to complete an issue.

## Boundary

A2 tightens story selection and image provenance only. It does not authorize Preview dispatch, Pages changes, production deployment, unattended publication, a daily GitHub Actions schedule, copying third-party media, private-data movement, or HarryTone changes.
