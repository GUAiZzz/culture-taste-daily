# Culture & Taste Daily — North Star

## What this is

Culture & Taste Daily is a daily bilingual digital culture magazine. It should feel closer to an authored independent magazine or zine than a PM dashboard, consulting report, trend database, generic news feed, or fixed blog template.

Chinese carries the complete editorial argument. English complements it with a title, deck, and closing abstract without adding unsupported facts.

Coverage may include fashion, streetwear, luxury, sneakers, accessories, fragrance, beauty, collaborations, art, design, architecture, exhibitions, retail, hospitality, music, film, photography, publishing, youth culture, city scenes, subcultures, emerging aesthetics, and meaningful technology × culture intersections.

## Reference architecture

References are teachers or discovery sources, never templates. Today's verified material, editorial position, HarryTone, and this North Star remain the authority. A reference cannot invent the issue's point of view.

The operating rules and pre-output gates live in `docs/REFERENCE_SYSTEM.md`. The evidence behind this change lives in `docs/REFERENCE_AUDIT_2026-08-23.md`.

### Core teacher: Codrops / Webzibition

Reference: https://tympanus.net/codrops/webzibition/page/2/

Role: authorship, world-building, concept concentration.

Learn from it:
- one dominant visual idea;
- typography behaving like a voice;
- a page experienced through time, not merely scrolled;
- asymmetric composition;
- dramatic density changes;
- interruptions and visual silence;
- image-led sequencing;
- spatial ideas and intentional motion.

Ask: **What does this issue believe a digital magazine should feel like today?**

Every issue should identify one dominant visual action that comes from that day's material. Examples of decision scale include type cutting through image fields, a continuous image spine, blackout-to-whiteout progression, collage resolving into a reading column, or modules opening into full bleed. These are examples of ambition, not templates to rotate.

Do not add WebGL or motion merely to look advanced. Spectacle cannot outrank reading clarity.

### Core teacher: Mobbin

Reference: https://mobbin.com/discover/apps/web/latest

Role: real product grammar, reader access, and interaction clarity.

Learn from it:
- understandable navigation and reading direction;
- predictable interactions and state changes;
- focus, hover, touch, and keyboard behavior;
- expectation setting and progress visibility;
- accessibility;
- robust states and clear links;
- product-level interaction polish.

Do not use Mobbin as the aesthetic or import commercial conversion logic into an editorial reading experience. Use it as the constraint: **the experimental magazine must still behave like a real product.**

### Specialist teacher: Behance UI/UX

Reference: https://www.behance.net/galleries/ui-ux/ui-ux

Role: presentation, story sequencing, dramatic viewing arc.

Learn from it:
- how a project introduces itself;
- cover → system/context → detail → climax → release;
- hierarchy through major scale changes;
- image ratios and pacing;
- dense and quiet zones;
- a memorable late-page integrated moment;
- coherence across a long artifact.

Do not copy portfolio mockups, gratuitous gradients, giant type, decorative 3D, or presentation polish that disguises weak editorial content.

Test: **If the presentation decoration disappeared, would the editorial idea still be strong?**

Behance is not an equal aesthetic input. Use it when the issue needs help with a deliberate long-form viewing arc. Do not copy portfolio mockups, fake metrics, decorative process diagrams, or a sequence whose main job is proving the designer's ability.

### Specialist teacher: AWGE

Reference: https://www.awge.com/home

Role: cultural attitude, internet-native world-building, brand-as-environment, and anti-template identity.

Learn from it:
- identity expressed through the whole environment rather than a logo plus UI kit;
- a small navigation system that remains inside the world;
- media, people, rules, status messages, and commerce carrying one attitude;
- deliberate roughness and surprise that still feel owned;
- the choice to hand commercial tasks to a clearer product surface when needed.

Do not copy its CRT, pixel type, glitch, star field, nostalgia, opacity, or subcultural signs. Consult AWGE only when today's material has enough cultural specificity to earn an environment of its own.

### Inspiration libraries: SiteInspire and Recent

References:
- https://www.siteinspire.com/websites
- https://recent.design/websites

Role: focused discovery, not instruction.

Use SiteInspire for composition, typography, grid, subject/style taxonomy, and historical precedents. Use Recent for current cross-category discovery and freshness scanning.

Do not treat screenshot taste, tags, popularity, or recency as proof that a direction belongs in the issue.

### Detail-level learning

Culture & Taste learns from how a specific work connects content, narrative, form, and reader behavior. It does not learn only from the appearance of an inspiration site's homepage.

Homepages, gallery pages, rankings, category feeds, and tags are discovery surfaces. After the editorial position is locked, an accepted reference must descend to the relevant project detail and, when available, the live work or the creator's case-study article. Every issue must reopen and verify at least one such detail-level source. Keep what was directly observed, what the creator explicitly stated, and what the editor inferred as separate evidence.

If a detail source is inaccessible, record the limit and narrow or reject the claim. Do not invent the missing rationale. A strong issue may use no visual reference when no relevant case clears this bar.

## How the layers work together

- Today's material asks: **What can this issue responsibly say, and what world does that material support?**
- Codrops asks: **Does the issue have an authored digital idea?**
- Mobbin asks: **Can a real reader understand and use it?**
- Behance asks when needed: **Does the issue unfold with a deliberate sequence?**
- AWGE asks when needed: **Does the issue own a cultural attitude rather than wear a style?**
- SiteInspire and Recent ask: **Which precedents are worth inspecting for this specific unresolved problem?**

Do not average the sources together. Lock the editorial position first, then select at most one primary conceptual teacher, one sequencing/composition reference, and one usability constraint by default. Fewer is valid.

- Codrops = authorship.
- Mobbin = functional constraint.
- Behance = sequence specialist.
- AWGE = cultural-attitude specialist.
- SiteInspire / Recent = discovery libraries.

These roles describe how a source may help after a specific work has been inspected. They do not make a source's index page an art-direction authority.

## Daily variation

Culture & Taste Daily must not reuse one visual template every day.

Do not default to the same beige paper, serif cover, blue/red accents, story cards, two-column layout, image ratios, hero composition, or scroll behavior.

Each issue should privately decide at least:

- dominant mood;
- editorial position;
- unresolved design problem;
- primary reference role and transferable principle;
- issue-specific translation and do-not-copy boundary;
- background logic;
- palette logic;
- type-scale logic;
- image behavior;
- page density and tempo;
- core visual action;
- cover composition;
- desktop interaction mechanic;
- Behance viewing arc;
- mobile translation;
- Mobbin usability constraint;
- quiet ending.

Before designing a new issue, compare up to the previous seven valid issues. Compare dominant background, cover composition, core visual action, typography contrast, image treatment, story-module rhythm, interaction mechanic, location of the main visual climax, and the mechanism signature defined in `docs/REFERENCE_SYSTEM.md`.

Do not repeat the same dominant background logic, cover composition, or core interaction by default. Repetition is allowed when today's content genuinely demands it; record why.

The purpose is to prevent unconscious template reuse, not to force gimmicks.

## FORM / MOTION / NARRATIVE / FUNCTION

Classify major decisions privately under:

- FORM
- MOTION
- NARRATIVE
- FUNCTION

Innovation cannot exist only in FORM. NARRATIVE and FUNCTION must always be solved deliberately. MOTION is optional.

A static page with strong authorship is better than a weak page with complicated animation.

## HarryTone relationship

HarryTone is not a visual skin.

HarryTone primarily governs:
- judgment;
- reasoning;
- truth boundaries;
- speaking position;
- editorial voice;
- anti-AI behavior;
- reasoning closure.

Core reasoning shape:

`concrete anchor → bounded judgment → evidence/relation → consequence, tension, or next development`

Visual authorship is governed by the Culture & Taste publication system layered on top of HarryTone. This separation should remain explicit unless a future dedicated HarryTone Visual Editorial branch is created.

## The system must remain improvable

This document describes the strongest system we know today. It is not permanent aesthetic doctrine.

If a clearly better approach is discovered:
1. explain why it is better;
2. identify which current assumption it replaces;
3. verify it against editorial quality, usability, reliability, accessibility, and maintainability;
4. propose the improvement before changing architecture.

The goal is not perfect compliance with today's document. The goal is to keep making Culture & Taste Daily a better authored digital publication.
